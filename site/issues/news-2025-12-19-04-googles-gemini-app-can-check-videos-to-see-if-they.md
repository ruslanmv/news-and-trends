## What Happened

Google's Gemini app has expanded its AI verification feature to videos made with Google AI models. This feature allows users to ask Gemini if an uploaded video is AI-generated by asking, "Was this generated using Google AI?"

The feature uses the company's proprietary watermark called SynthID to analyze the video's visuals and audio for the presence of this watermark. If the watermark is detected, the video is considered AI-generated.

## Why It Matters

The new AI verification feature for videos made with Google AI is significant because it allows users to quickly and easily determine the authenticity of the video. This feature can help to combat the spread of misinformation and protect users from being misled by AI-generated videos.

## Context & Background

The feature is a major addition to the already existing AI capabilities offered by Google Gemini. This feature is useful for a wide range of users, including content creators, consumers, and anyone interested in learning more about AI.

## What to Watch Next

The release of this new AI verification feature is a major milestone in the development of AI transparency. As AI technology continues to advance, it will become increasingly important for users to have tools to understand and verify the authenticity of AI-generated content.
