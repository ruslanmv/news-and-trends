## What Happened


IBM and University of Chicago researchers have achieved a groundbreaking milestone in the field of quantum computing. Their recent achievement involved solving a classically intractable problem in just 15 minutes using a remarkable 70-qubit quantum computer. This remarkable result stands as a testament to the power of quantum computing and its potential applications across a diverse range of industries.


## Why It Matters


This groundbreaking achievement has significant implications for several reasons. First, it showcases the immense potential of quantum computing to solve complex problems that are intractable for classical computers. This advancement could pave the way for groundbreaking innovations in diverse fields such as medicine, materials science, finance, and drug discovery.
