## What Happened

Google unveiled its deepest AI research agent yet on the same day as OpenAI dropped its landmark GPT-5.2. This groundbreaking development allows developers to embed Google's Deep Research tool, based on Gemini 3 Pro, into their own applications. This integration signifies a significant milestone in AI development, paving the way for more complex and efficient AI integration across various sectors.

This groundbreaking integration holds immense potential to revolutionize AI development by enabling developers to access a vast range of capabilities directly within their applications.
