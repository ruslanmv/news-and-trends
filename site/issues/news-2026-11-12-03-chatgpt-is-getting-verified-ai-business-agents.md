## What Happened

ChatGPT is launching a new feature called "Business Agents" that allows businesses to connect with verified "Business Agents" on the platform. These agents represent various brands and offer assistance to users in comparison shopping, product research, and other business-related tasks.

The feature is currently available to "eligible" e-commerce and online service businesses in the United States and is part of a suite of business-focused features being announced today in beta.

## Why It Matters

The "Business Agents" feature is significant because it addresses the growing need for personalized customer service. By partnering with established businesses, ChatGPT aims to provide users with access to expert advice and support tailored to their specific business needs. This can lead to increased conversions, improved customer satisfaction, and higher revenue for participating businesses.

## Context & Background

The launch of the "Business Agents" feature comes at a time when e-commerce and online businesses are rapidly evolving. As consumers become more accustomed to online shopping, businesses are looking for ways to enhance the customer experience and build trust. ChatGPT's "Business Agents" offer a unique and valuable solution to this challenge by connecting businesses with potential customers who can provide guidance and support.

## What to Watch Next

The launch of the "Business Agents" feature is a major milestone in ChatGPT's ongoing efforts to expand its business reach and offer innovative solutions to various industries. The feature is expected to be widely adopted by businesses of all sizes and industries, helping them streamline their operations and enhance customer satisfaction.
