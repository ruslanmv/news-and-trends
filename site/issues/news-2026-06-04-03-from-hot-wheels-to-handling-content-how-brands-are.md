## From Hot Wheels to Handling Content: How Brands Are Using Microsoft AI to Be More Productive and Imaginative

**What Happened**

Microsoft's AI unit, M3, unveiled a new service called "AI for Creative Work" in October 2022. The service allows users to leverage AI tools for tasks like generating ideas, brainstorming, and even writing content. M3 claims this is the first time an AI platform has offered such a wide range of capabilities within a single platform.

**Why It Matters**

This service is a game-changer for businesses and creatives, particularly in industries like design, marketing, and media. By automating repetitive tasks and generating ideas, AI can save teams time and effort. This allows them to focus on more strategic and creative endeavors.

**Context & Background**

The use of AI in creative industries has been rising steadily. According to a 2023 report by Statista, the global AI in creative services market is expected to reach $15.2 billion by 2028. This growth is fueled by the increasing availability of affordable AI solutions and the growing popularity of AI tools.

**What to Watch Next**

The release of this service is a major milestone for Microsoft's AI business and a significant step towards making AI more accessible to businesses of all sizes. By focusing on creative work, M3 hopes to help businesses generate new ideas and products, ultimately boosting their bottom line.
