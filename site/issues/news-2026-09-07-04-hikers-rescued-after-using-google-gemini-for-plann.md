## What Happened

Google Gemini, an AI-powered travel planning tool, advised hikers to bring only a portion of their food and water, due to an expected power outage in the area. The sheriff's office confirmed that the hikers were rescued after being stranded on the trail due to the outage.

## Why It Matters

The incident highlights the importance of planning and being aware of potential disruptions. It also emphasizes the vulnerability of outdoor enthusiasts who may not have the necessary resources or knowledge to handle unexpected situations.

## Context & Background

The hikers were enjoying a hike in a remote area when they received the alert from Google Gemini. The tool had estimated an extended power outage, potentially jeopardizing their safety.

## What to Watch Next

The hikers are expected to receive medical attention and be checked for injuries. The sheriff's office is monitoring the situation and assessing the impact on the entire trail users. It's still uncertain if the hike will resume as planned.
