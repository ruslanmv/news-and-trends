## What Happened

Google unveiled a new feature called "Talk Like a Graph" for their large language model, LaMDA. This feature allows users to interact with and understand LaMDA by generating human-like text based on visual patterns.

The announcement promises a more intuitive and engaging way to interact with AI. Users can create visual concepts, and LaMDA will translate them into natural language, enabling a deeper understanding of the visual context.

## Why It Matters

This feature has the potential to revolutionize the way we interact with AI. By allowing users to express their thoughts and ideas visually, LaMDA can better understand and respond to them. This could lead to improved communication and collaboration between humans and AI systems.
