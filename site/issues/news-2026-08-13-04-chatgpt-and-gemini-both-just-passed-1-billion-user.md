## What Happened

The Verge announced that both ChatGPT and Gemini have reached 1 billion users each month. This is the 14th time that a Google product has hit 1 billion users. Google CEO Sundar Pichai announced the milestone on Twitter, stating that a billion people use Gemini every month.

This significant achievement highlights the growing popularity of AI applications and the immense potential of these technologies to impact numerous industries.

## Why It Matters

This news is significant because it underscores the immense reach and impact of AI applications like ChatGPT and Gemini. These tools have the potential to revolutionize how we interact with technology, automate tasks, and solve complex problems.

The development of ChatGPT and Gemini has opened up new opportunities for businesses and consumers alike. ChatGPT can be used for a wide range of tasks, including language translation, content creation, and customer service automation. Gemini is a versatile platform that can be used for a variety of purposes, including task scheduling, fitness tracking, and language learning.
