## What Happened

Barret Zoph, co-founder of Thinking Machines Lab, has joined Google. The news, published by TechCrunch, indicates Zoph's brief stint at OpenAI has brought him to Google's tech hub in Mountain View.

## Why It Matters

Zoph's departure from OpenAI signifies a significant shift in the AI landscape. He co-founded OpenAI alongside Mira Murati and served as the startup's CTO. His arrival at Google brings valuable experience and expertise to the tech giant's AI team.

## Context & Background

Zoph's co-founding of OpenAI in 2015 was hailed as a major milestone in the AI industry. The company developed machine learning software for various applications, including natural language processing, computer vision, and robotics. OpenAI's technology has been used in products and services by major companies like Netflix, Amazon, and Facebook.

## What to Watch Next

Zoph's arrival at Google will undoubtedly fuel the development of AI products and services. His expertise and experience will contribute to the company's ongoing efforts in tackling complex challenges in the AI field.
