## What Happened

OpenAI has announced that its free and Go tiers will be expanding to allow users to have unlimited text chats with the ChatGPT chatbot. This means that users will no longer be subject to rate limits and can engage in extended conversations with the AI without facing any limitations.

## Why It Matters

This change is significant for several reasons. First, it will provide users with greater flexibility and convenience when interacting with ChatGPT. Users will be able to engage in longer and more in-depth conversations without being constrained by rate limits. Second, this move is expected to increase the popularity of ChatGPT and attract a wider range of users, including businesses and organizations. Finally, it could also have a positive impact on the AI industry as a whole, as it will make it easier for developers to create and maintain chatbots without being limited by rate restrictions.

## Context & Background

ChatGPT has gained immense popularity since its release in 2023. It has become a widely used tool for a wide range of tasks, from customer service to education to entertainment. However, due to rate limits, users have often had to be patient and wait for their turn to chat with ChatGPT.

In recent months, OpenAI has been working to address this issue and increase the scalability of ChatGPT. The latest update to ChatGPT is a testament to this effort, and it is expected to have a significant impact on the future of AI technology.
