Google Maps has just introduced a new feature called "Gemini" which is designed to help users plan their day in a more personalized and efficient way. Gemini uses various data points, including your daily schedule, work commitments, and personal interests, to generate personalized suggestions and recommendations.

According to the Verge, Gemini has been in development for over a year, but it's only recently been made available to users. The feature is currently in beta testing but is expected to launch globally soon.
