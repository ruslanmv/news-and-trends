## What Happened

Google has just introduced a new feature called "auto browse" to Chrome, which can perform multi-step tasks on your behalf. The Gemini AI-powered capability is coming to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the US, and can do things like research hotel and flight costs, schedule appointments, fill out online forms, manage subscriptions, and more.

The new feature is accessible to users who have upgraded to Google AI Pro or Ultra subscriptions. It is not accessible to users with basic Chrome subscriptions.

## Why It Matters

The auto browse feature is a major step forward in AI-powered automation. It allows users to perform a wide range of tasks without having to manually enter all of the details. This can save users time and effort, and can also improve the accuracy of their work.

## Context & Background

The auto browse feature is a relatively new addition to the Chrome browser. It was first announced in January 2026, and it is currently available to users in the US. The feature is still in its early stages, but it has already been well-received by users.
