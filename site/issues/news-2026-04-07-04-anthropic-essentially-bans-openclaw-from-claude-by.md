## What Happened

Anthropic has just announced a new policy change that will significantly impact Claude subscribers. Starting April 4th at 3 PM ET, users will no longer be able to use their Claude subscription limits for third-party harnesses including OpenClaw. This means that OpenClaw will be more expensive for users who rely on Claude for access to this feature.

The change was announced in an email sent to Claude users on Friday evening. The email did not provide any additional details about the reasons behind the decision, but it did say that the change was made to ensure the sustainability of both Anthropic and Claude.

## Why It Matters

This change is significant because it will make OpenClaw more expensive for users. Many users rely on OpenClaw to access their Claude account and manage their subscriptions. This change will likely lead to a loss of revenue for both Anthropic and Claude.

## Context & Background

Anthropic and Claude are two major companies in the tech industry that are closely tied together. Anthropic's Claude is a subscription service that allows users to access a wide range of AI-powered tools and services. Claude is used by many major tech companies, including Google and Microsoft.

This change is also significant because it comes at a time when the tech industry is facing a number of challenges. The industry is facing competition from other companies, such as Amazon and Microsoft, which are investing heavily in AI. This competition is putting pressure on traditional tech companies to find new ways to generate revenue.

## What to Watch Next

It is expected that Anthropic will announce further details about the OpenClaw subscription changes in the coming weeks. It is also possible that Claude will offer alternative solutions to compensate for the loss of functionality.
