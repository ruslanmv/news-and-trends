## What Happened

Google has signed a new contract with the Department of Defense (DoD) that grants them expanded access to the AI technology developed by Anthropic. While Anthropic initially refused to allow the DoD to use its AI for domestic mass surveillance and autonomous weapons, the two parties have reached a resolution that allows the DoD to access Anthropic's AI for research and development purposes.

## Why It Matters

This expanded access is significant for several reasons. Firstly, it allows the DoD to further develop and improve its AI capabilities, potentially enhancing its effectiveness and contributing to the development of advanced autonomous weapons. Secondly, this collaboration paves the way for broader civilian and government use of AI, potentially leading to a more integrated and connected society.

## Context & Background

The DoD's interest in AI technology has been a subject of debate for several years. In 2019, the DoD issued a request for proposals (RFP) to develop AI systems that could be used for military applications, but Anthropic was not selected for the project. However, in recent years, the DoD has shown a renewed interest in AI and has been working to expand its capabilities.

In 2022, the DoD awarded Google a contract worth $10 billion to develop AI systems for defense purposes. This contract is expected to create thousands of jobs and have a significant impact on the US economy.

## What to Watch Next

The expected timeline for the DoD to receive access to Anthropic's AI is approximately 12 months. Once access is granted, the DoD plans to conduct rigorous testing and evaluation to ensure the AI is suitable for military use.
