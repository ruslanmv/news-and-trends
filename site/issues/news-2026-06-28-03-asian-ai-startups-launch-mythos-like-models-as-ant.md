## What Happened

The Asian AI startups are facing an obstacle in their quest to launch their Mythos-like models due to the ongoing export ban in the United States. Despite the challenges, several companies are striving to break free from this restrictive trade barrier.

According to TechCrunch, the recent developments have forced the founders to reconsider their strategy, as they may never be able to fully recover the enormous market they had hoped for. The export ban has put immense pressure on these startups, as they cannot export their products to the US market.

## Why It Matters

The Mythos-like models developed by these Asian startups offer a breakthrough in the field of AI, with capabilities that surpass those of current state-of-the-art models. If successful, these models could have a profound impact on the AI landscape, showcasing the immense potential of the Asian AI ecosystem.

## Context & Background

The rapid advancement of AI technology has sparked significant interest globally, with countries like China and the US investing heavily in research and development. The export ban has created a bottleneck that limits the flow of cutting-edge AI models to the US market. This has forced Asian AI startups to look for alternative pathways to reach US investors and consumers.

## What to Watch Next

The future of these startups is uncertain, as it remains unclear when the export ban will be lifted. If the ban is lifted, these companies have the opportunity to showcase their models to a global audience and establish themselves as leaders in the AI industry. However, if the ban remains in place, they may need to find alternative solutions to achieve sustainable growth.
