## Using AI to expand global access to reliable flood forecasts

**What Happened**

Google AI Blog announced the development of a machine learning model that can expand global access to reliable flood forecasts. This model, named "FloodAI," uses a combination of data science and artificial intelligence to analyze historical and real-time data from various sources, including weather patterns, satellite imagery, and hydrological data. By identifying patterns and trends in these data, FloodAI can predict flood risks with greater accuracy and automate flood monitoring.

**Why It Matters**

FloodAI has the potential to revolutionize flood forecasting by providing timely and accurate predictions in areas that are currently underserved by traditional forecasting methods. This will allow for early flood detection and mitigation, resulting in significant benefits for communities and infrastructure.

**Context & Background**

Flooding poses a major threat to human life and property, particularly in low-lying areas and coastal regions. Despite advancements in flood monitoring technology, reaching isolated or inaccessible areas remains challenging due to limited access to real-time data and infrastructure.

**What to Watch Next**

The launch of FloodAI is a major milestone in addressing this challenge. The model is expected to significantly improve flood forecast accuracy, providing valuable insights to governments, disaster management agencies, and communities. The model will also enable the development of more resilient infrastructure and flood mitigation strategies.
