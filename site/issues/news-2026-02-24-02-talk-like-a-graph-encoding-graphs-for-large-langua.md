LaMDA is a large language model (LLM) developed by Google AI. LLMs are a type of artificial intelligence (AI) that is trained on massive datasets of text and code. This allows them to learn the meaning of language and to generate new text that is similar to the training data.

LLMs have a wide range of potential applications, including natural language processing (NLP), machine translation, and question answering. However, the development of LLMs has also raised concerns about privacy and bias.
