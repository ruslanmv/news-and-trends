## What Happened

Google is testing a new AI-driven shopping experience called "Gemini" for select Flipkart customers in India. The limited test covers a limited range of products and users, with a broader rollout planned for later in October.

## Why It Matters

The launch of Gemini is significant because it marks Google's entry into the burgeoning e-commerce space in India. The company has been steadily expanding its reach in the country, and this move will help them gain a foothold in the highly competitive online retail market.

## Context & Background

The Indian e-commerce market is expected to reach a value of \$1.2 trillion by 2026, making it one of the fastest-growing markets in the world. As a major e-commerce player, Google's entry into India will further intensify competition.

## What to Watch Next

The success of the Gemini test will determine Google's future plans for the Indian market. If the test is successful, Google is expected to roll out Gemini to a wider audience in the coming months. This could potentially disrupt the e-commerce landscape in India and create significant revenue for Google.
