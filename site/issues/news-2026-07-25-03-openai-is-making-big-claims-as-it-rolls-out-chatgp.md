## What Happened

OpenAI's ChatGPT Health is now available to everyone in the United States. This means that more people will be able to connect their medical records and health-tracking information to the chatbot.

The company's models "are now capable of reasoning at levels that are better than clinician level," according to Ashley Alexander, OpenAI's vice president of health product. This means that ChatGPT Health can now provide medical advice, diagnose diseases, and recommend treatment plans.

## Why It Matters

ChatGPT Health has the potential to revolutionize healthcare. By giving patients more control over their health data, it can help them to take a more active role in managing their health. This can lead to better outcomes and reduced healthcare costs.

## Context & Background

ChatGPT Health is the latest in a series of AI-powered medical tools that are being developed by OpenAI. The company has already launched ChatGPT for medical diagnosis and ChatGPT for drug discovery.

ChatGPT Health is also important because it is a major step forward in the development of AI for healthcare. By making AI more accessible and affordable, OpenAI is making it possible for more people to benefit from the benefits of AI in healthcare.

## What to Watch Next

The future of ChatGPT Health is bright. The company plans to continue to develop and improve the chatbot, and it hopes to make it available in more countries in the future. Additionally, OpenAI is working on a number of other AI projects for healthcare, including a medical chatbot that can provide personalized health advice.
