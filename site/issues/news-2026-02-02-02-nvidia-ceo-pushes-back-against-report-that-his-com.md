## What Happened

Nvidia's Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Jensen Huang has denied a recent report claiming friction between his company and OpenAI. The report, which was published by TechCrunch, suggested that the $100 billion investment in OpenAI may have stalled.

Huang stated in a statement that the report was "nonsense" and that Nvidia had "no issues" with OpenAI. He added that the company is committed to working with OpenAI and that they have a "strong partnership" in place.

This denial comes despite the fact that the report has garnered significant attention within the tech industry. Some analysts believe that the report could indicate a shift in the relationship between Nvidia and OpenAI, which could have a major impact on the future of artificial intelligence (AI).

## Why It Matters

The recent report's publication is significant because it could have a major impact on the AI industry. Nvidia's OpenAI investment has been a major source of revenue and innovation for the company. If the report is true, it could mean that Nvidia is reconsidering its commitment to OpenAI. This could have a significant impact on the future of AI development and the entire tech industry.

Nvidia's OpenAI partnership has been one of the most successful collaborations in the industry.
