## What Happened

IBM and University of Chicago researchers have achieved a breakthrough in quantum computing by successfully solving a classically intractable problem in 15 minutes. The system, which utilized 70 error-corrected logical qubits, demonstrated remarkable performance and statistical evidence supporting the result.

## Why It Matters

This groundbreaking achievement has significant implications for various industries, including:

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Quantum computing opens doors to developing more advanced AI models with superior capabilities and faster processing times.
- **Drug Discovery:** The problem tackled could significantly accelerate drug discovery, accelerating the development of new treatments and therapies.
- **Cryptography:** Quantum computing presents a potential threat to current cryptographic systems, requiring the development of new and more robust encryption methods.

## Context & Background

This achievement follows a series of recent breakthroughs in quantum computing, including the demonstration of quantum supremacy in 2021 by Google and the solution of a different classically intractable problem in 2022 by Microsoft. The complexity of this task is further highlighted by the fact that it was intractable for classical computers to solve.

## What to Watch Next

The next major milestone in this research is the development and deployment of a quantum computer capable of solving problems that were once deemed impossible. The anticipated timeline for this development is approximately 5-7 years, with first qubit delivery expected in 2028.

This groundbreaking achievement signifies the rapid progress and potential of quantum computing. While challenges remain, the potential benefits of this technology are vast, paving the way for transformative advancements in various sectors and creating a quantum future.
