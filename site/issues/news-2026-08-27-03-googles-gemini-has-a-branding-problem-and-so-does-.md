## What Happened

Google's Gemini, a consumer AI app used to organize and manage tasks, has come under fire for its confusing and cluttered user interface. The app has been criticized for requiring users to learn the app's product architecture before they can effectively use it.

This lack of intuitiveness has resulted in frustration and discouragement among users, leading to a decline in its popularity. The app has received numerous negative reviews on the App Store, with many users complaining about their inability to figure out basic features.

## Why It Matters

The confusing UI of Gemini poses a significant challenge to both new and experienced users. This issue can lead to misunderstandings, wasted time, and a diminished user experience. By making the app more user-friendly, Google can improve user satisfaction and retention.

## Context & Background

Launched in 2018, Gemini was initially hailed as a groundbreaking tool for managing personal tasks and reminders. However, its success has been overshadowed by its confusing design. In recent months, Google has been working to address the issue, but the app remains frustrating for many users.

The launch of a redesigned Gemini is expected in the coming months. This update will be eagerly anticipated by users seeking a more user-friendly and efficient way to manage their tasks.
