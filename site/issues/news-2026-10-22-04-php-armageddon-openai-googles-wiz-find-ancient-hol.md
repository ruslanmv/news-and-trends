## What Happened

OpenAI and Google's AI subsidiary, "Wiz", have both found ancient holes in the walls of some data centers, raising concerns about the security of their AI systems. The discovery highlights the vulnerability of AI infrastructure to attacks, even those designed to be highly secure.

Both companies have acknowledged the issue but have assured the public that the attacks are not related to their core AI research or products. The holes were discovered through routine maintenance checks, and the data centers have been secured to prevent further incidents.

However, the news has caused a stir in the AI community, with many experts expressing their concern about the potential impact on AI development and deployment. Some argue that the attack could hinder the advancement of AI and prevent the development of more advanced and reliable systems.

## Why It Matters

The recent attacks on OpenAI and Google's Wiz raise serious questions about the security of AI infrastructure. Such vulnerabilities could allow malicious actors to gain access to sensitive AI data, which could be used for malicious purposes such as creating deepfakes, spreading misinformation, or launching targeted attacks.

The potential consequences of these attacks cannot be understated. The development of AI has already led to a number of breakthroughs, from facial recognition and natural language processing to self-driving cars and drug discovery. However, it also raises concerns about the potential for AI to be used for harmful purposes.

## Context & Background

The recent attacks on OpenAI and Google's Wiz come at a time of heightened security concerns in the AI industry. In recent months, there have been a number of high-profile security breaches and attacks targeting AI companies and research institutions.

The recent attacks are also a reminder that AI systems are complex and interconnected, making them vulnerable to a variety of attacks. As AI systems become more sophisticated, it is increasingly important to consider the security risks associated with them.

## What to Watch Next

The cybersecurity community is closely monitoring the situation and will continue to provide updates as more information becomes available. Experts will be keeping an eye on the development of the AI industry and will be working to develop new safeguards to protect AI systems from attacks.
