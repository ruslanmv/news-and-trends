## What Happened

Google's AI tool, Opal, is now available for use within the Gemini web app. Opal allows users to build custom AI-powered mini apps directly within their Gemini accounts. This integration signifies a significant advancement in user-driven AI integration within web platforms.

## Why It Matters

The introduction of Opal represents a major step forward in personalized AI experiences. By empowering users with the ability to create their own AI applications, Gemini aims to revolutionize the way individuals interact with technology, offering a more tailored and engaging experience.

## Context & Background

The announcement comes amid a surge in interest in AI integration across various platforms. As AI becomes increasingly accessible, companies are seeking innovative ways to leverage its power to enhance user experiences. Gemini's collaboration with Google demonstrates the growing influence of AI in the tech industry, where seamless integration and user-driven applications are becoming increasingly crucial.

## What to Watch Next

The integration of Opal is expected to have a significant impact on the user base of Gemini. Additionally, the tool's accessibility within Gemini suggests that it could become a valuable resource for individuals seeking to expand their AI knowledge and skills.
