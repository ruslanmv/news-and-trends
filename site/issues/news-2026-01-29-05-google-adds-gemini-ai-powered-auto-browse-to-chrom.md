## What Happened

Google has recently launched a new AI-powered feature called "auto browse" within the Chrome browser. This feature is available to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the United States and will allow users to perform various multi-step tasks on their behalf, including researching hotel and flight costs, scheduling appointments, filling out online forms, managing subscriptions, and more.

## Why It Matters

The auto browse feature is a significant milestone in AI-powered browser tools. This feature will enhance user productivity and efficiency by streamlining the process of completing tasks that would otherwise require navigating multiple websites and entering information manually. The feature is also a testament to Google's commitment to innovation and providing users with the latest advancements in AI technology.

## Context & Background

The auto browse feature is a relatively recent addition to the Chrome browser, having been unveiled at the Google I/O developer conference in June 2023. This feature is part of Google's ongoing efforts to expand the capabilities of the Chrome browser and provide users with more functionalities. The feature is also a significant development in the field of AI-powered browser tools, as it demonstrates Google's commitment to pushing the boundaries of what is possible with AI.
