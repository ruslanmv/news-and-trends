## What Happened

The United Nations (UN) has announced a partnership with Google to make its global development data ready for artificial intelligence (AI) agents. The initiative aims to address the challenges and opportunities presented by the increasing use of AI and the need for accurate data to train and operate AI systems.

The UN test, conducted by UNICEF, demonstrated that leading AI models struggled to accurately retrieve essential data on global development, highlighting the need for a more comprehensive and standardized approach to data collection and management.

## Why It Matters

This partnership is significant because it will provide a standardized and reliable source of data for AI development and deployment. By ensuring that AI agents have access to accurate and comprehensive development data, the UN aims to foster innovation and accelerate the development of AI solutions that can address global challenges such as poverty, hunger, and climate change.

## Context & Background

The UN has been a leading advocate for data-driven approaches to development. In recent years, the organization has launched several initiatives to promote data transparency and access, including the Global Data Compact and the Open Data for Development Initiative.

This partnership with Google is a testament to the UN's commitment to addressing the challenges posed by AI and to ensuring that AI development is inclusive and equitable.

## What to Watch Next

The UN plans to work closely with Google and other stakeholders to develop a comprehensive data governance framework that will ensure the quality, security, and privacy of global development data. The framework is expected to be finalized and implemented within the next two years.
