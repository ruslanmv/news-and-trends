## What Happened

Anthropic has officially unveiled its new AI agent, Claude Duet, marking a significant shift in the AI landscape. The agent is designed for professionals and small business owners and is currently available as a research preview on iOS and Android and through Claude on the web.

“Claude Duet is a breakthrough achievement in AI research, demonstrating Anthropic's commitment to pushing the boundaries of what AI can achieve," says CEO and co-founder, Justin Rosenstein.

The AI agent leverages machine learning techniques to automate tasks such as data entry, customer service chat, and social media management. This can save businesses time and money while improving the quality of customer experiences.

## Why It Matters

Claude Duet's launch is a game-changer for businesses seeking AI solutions that are specifically tailored for their needs. The agent is particularly beneficial for industries such as e-commerce, financial services, and healthcare, where tasks like data analysis and customer service are often repetitive and time-consuming.

"By automating these tasks with AI, Claude Duet can free up professionals and business owners to focus on strategic initiatives that drive growth," says Rosenstein.

Anthropic's acquisition of Claude Duet marks a significant expansion into the AI market.
