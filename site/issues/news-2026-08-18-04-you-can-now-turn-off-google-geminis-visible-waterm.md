## What Happened

Google has recently introduced a new feature that allows users to remove visible watermarks from their AI-generated images, videos, and music. With this update, users can toggle off a new "Media watermark" setting in Gemini and Google's AI video generator, Flow. When toggled off, Google will remove the "sparkle" watermark that appears in the bottom-right corner of the generated content.

## Why It Matters

This new feature is significant because it gives users more control over how their content is used. By allowing users to remove watermarks, Google is removing a potential distraction and allowing them to use their AI creations for more creative and professional purposes. This feature also opens up new possibilities for businesses and organizations who use AI for various purposes, such as marketing, advertising, and content creation.

## Context & Background

This feature is part of Google's ongoing effort to improve the safety and privacy of its users. In recent years, the company has been under increasing pressure from privacy advocates and regulators to protect user data. The introduction of this new watermark removal feature is a step towards addressing this pressure and giving users more control over their data.

## What to Watch Next

The exact timeline for the full implementation of this feature is not yet known. However, Google has said that it will announce the feature in the near future. Once it is released, users can expect to be able to toggle it off in Gemini and Flow settings.
