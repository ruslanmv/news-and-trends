## What Happened

Google Gemini, a travel planning and decision-making app, advised hikers to bring only half the food and water they initially planned for their hike. The app provided estimated calorie and hydration needs based on the terrain and estimated hiking time. However, the hikers ignored these recommendations and brought more food and water than they needed, leading to a near-extinction situation.

## Why It Matters

This incident highlights the importance of being mindful of the app's recommendations and using them as a starting point for planning. Ignoring these recommendations resulted in a life-threatening situation, demonstrating the potential dangers associated with relying solely on app-generated plans.

## Context & Background

The incident occurred in a remote area with challenging terrain and weather conditions. Hikers were expected to be self-sufficient for the duration of the hike. The app's recommendations would have provided valuable guidance had they been followed.

## What to Watch Next

The sheriff's office is investigating the incident and will provide updates as more information becomes available. The app developers will also review the situation and make any necessary adjustments to their recommendations.
