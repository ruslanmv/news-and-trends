## What Happened

Anthropic's latest update to Claude AI allows the AI chatbot to generate custom charts, diagrams, and other visualizations during your conversation. This feature is a significant advancement for Anthropic's Claude, as it enables the AI to communicate more effectively and provide users with a more intuitive and engaging experience.

The ability to generate visual content also opens up a wide range of possibilities for use cases such as education, marketing, and scientific research. By providing users with visual representations of complex data and concepts, Claude can help to improve comprehension and retention.

## Context & Background

Anthropic's Claude is a large language model that has been trained on a massive amount of text and code. Claude is capable of generating human-quality text, images, and videos. However, until now, Claude has been unable to generate visual content.

This new feature is the first time that Claude has been able to generate visual content on its own. This is a major milestone for AI, as it opens up a wide range of possibilities for how AI can be used.
