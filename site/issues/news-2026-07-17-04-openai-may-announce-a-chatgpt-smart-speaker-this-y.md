## What Happened

OpenAI's ambitious project to develop a smart speaker powered by ChatGPT has taken a significant leap forward with the company revealing its first device is on the horizon. The device, expected to launch this year, will be a purely vocal smart speaker that utilizes a camera and additional sensors to understand its surroundings and respond accordingly.

The absence of a screen is a bold move by OpenAI, but it promises to be more user-friendly and compact compared to traditional smart speakers. The speaker will also utilize advanced natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML) for seamless communication with ChatGPT.

## Why It Matters

This launch marks a watershed moment for OpenAI and the AI industry at large. By introducing a smart speaker with ChatGPT, OpenAI aims to challenge Apple's dominance in the smart home market and offer a more accessible and affordable solution for consumers. This could also pave the way for the development of more innovative and advanced AI devices in the future.

## Context & Background

The announcement comes amidst a flurry of AI news, with Apple facing a lawsuit from OpenAI regarding its Siri and the use of AI in its devices. This legal battle reflects the increasing tension between the tech industry giants and the growing field of AI, with both sides striving for dominance.

The burgeoning AI market is experiencing exponential growth, with demand for AI-powered solutions reaching unprecedented levels. This launch by OpenAI is strategically positioned to capitalize on this growing market and establish its dominance in the smart home space.

## What to Watch Next

The development of this ChatGPT-powered smart speaker is expected to be swift, with the device potentially debuting before the end of 2023. The product will likely face stiff competition from Apple's existing Siri smart speaker, but OpenAI has committed significant resources to ensure its device stands out in the market.
