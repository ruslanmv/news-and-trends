## What Happened

OpenAI is planning to announce the release of a smart speaker powered by ChatGPT this year. The device will be the first open-source smart speaker, according to a report from Bloomberg. The speaker will use a camera and additional sensors to understand its environment and let you interact with ChatGPT through voice commands.

## Why It Matters

The open-source nature of the device represents a major shift in the way AI devices are developed and distributed. By allowing for transparency and collaboration, OpenAI hopes to foster trust and encourage wider adoption of AI technology.

## Context & Background

The announcement comes at a time when AI is rapidly gaining popularity. ChatGPT has become a household name, with its language-generating capabilities and ability to engage in meaningful conversations being leveraged in various industries, including education, healthcare, and customer service.

## What to Watch Next

The official launch of the ChatGPT smart speaker is expected to take place this year, with the device likely entering production later in the year. It is also important to note that the device will be open-source, meaning that its source code will be publicly available for scrutiny and modification.
