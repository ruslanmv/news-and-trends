## What Happened

The ChatGPT mobile app has received significant updates that enable Pro and Plus users to complete agentic tasks directly on their phones. This feature allows users to generate text, translate languages, and perform other cognitive tasks hands-free using voice commands.

## Why It Matters

This advancement is a major milestone for ChatGPT, expanding its capabilities beyond text generation and opening up a new range of possibilities for both consumers and businesses. By allowing users to perform these tasks without relying on a keyboard or mouse, ChatGPT's reach and accessibility are significantly increased.

## Context & Background

ChatGPT's popularity and influence have grown rapidly since its launch. The app has been downloaded over 1 billion times and is used by a wide range of people, from students and professionals to artists and entrepreneurs. As a result, the development of voice-based generative capabilities is a significant addition to the app's capabilities.

## What to Watch Next

The ChatGPT team is expected to continue developing and introducing new features in the future. It is also likely that the company will explore partnerships with other companies and institutions to offer its services to a wider range of clients.
