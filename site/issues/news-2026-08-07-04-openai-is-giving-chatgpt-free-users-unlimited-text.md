## What Happened

OpenAI has expanded the free tier for ChatGPT, allowing users to have unlimited text chats with the AI chatbot. Previously, users on the free tier were limited to a certain number of text chats per month.

This change is expected to benefit both ChatGPT users and businesses that rely on the chatbot for marketing and customer support. Users who exceed the previous monthly limit will no longer encounter rate limitations and have the freedom to engage in prolonged conversations with the AI.

## Why It Matters

This move is significant because it expands the accessibility of ChatGPT to a wider audience. By removing the monthly rate limit, OpenAI is enabling more users to explore and experiment with the capabilities of ChatGPT. This can lead to increased adoption of the AI, which could benefit various industries such as education, healthcare, and marketing.

## Context & Background

ChatGPT is a large language model (LLM) that has gained immense popularity since its release in 2023. LLMs are a type of artificial intelligence that is capable of understanding and generating human-like text. ChatGPT is trained on a massive dataset of text and code, and it has shown remarkable abilities in various language tasks, including language generation, translation, and question answering.

This latest update to ChatGPT's free tier is a testament to the company's commitment to making the AI accessible to as many people as possible. By expanding the free tier, OpenAI is increasing its potential reach and influence, which could have a significant impact on the future of AI development and adoption.
