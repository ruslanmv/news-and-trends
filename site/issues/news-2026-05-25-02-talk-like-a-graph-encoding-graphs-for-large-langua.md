## What Happened

Google has announced the release of a new tool called "Talk Like a Graph" that can encode and generate natural language text using graph representations. This tool has the potential to revolutionize how we interact with large language models (LLMs) by allowing us to communicate with them more naturally.

**Why It Matters**

The ability to interact with LLMs through graphs, rather than relying on complex text-based interfaces, could open up a wide range of possibilities. It could make it easier for users to understand and use LLMs, especially for those with limited reading or writing skills. It could also lead to more creative and engaging conversations between humans and LLMs.

**Context & Background**

Graph embeddings are a way of representing data as a graph, where nodes represent entities and edges represent relationships between them. These embeddings can be used to learn relationships between entities and to generate new text.

The announcement of Talk Like a Graph is a major milestone in the development of natural language processing (NLP). NLP is the study of computers and human language, and it is used to develop machines that can understand and generate human language.
