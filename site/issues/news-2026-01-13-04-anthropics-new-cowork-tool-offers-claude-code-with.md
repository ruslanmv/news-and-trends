## What Happened

Anthropic's new Cowork tool allows users to access Claude Code without requiring the original code itself. This innovative feature streamlines collaboration by enabling users to modify files through the Claude Desktop app without sharing the sensitive code.

## Why It Matters

This development is significant because it expands the accessibility and ease of use of Claude Code, potentially revolutionizing how businesses and individuals collaborate. By eliminating the need for users to possess the original code, Anthropic aims to simplify the onboarding process, increase efficiency, and foster greater collaboration.

## Context & Background

Anthropic's acquisition of Claude is a major step in expanding its reach and diversifying its offerings. This acquisition allows Anthropic to leverage the existing user base of Claude and provide a more comprehensive suite of solutions for its customers. Additionally, it provides Claude with access to Anthropic's extensive network and resources, further enhancing its functionality and capabilities.

## What to Watch Next

The release of Anthropic's new Cowork tool is highly anticipated by the tech community. The tool is expected to have a significant impact on the collaborative workflow of businesses and individuals. The company plans to release additional features and updates to the tool in the future, further expanding its capabilities and solidifying its position in the market.
