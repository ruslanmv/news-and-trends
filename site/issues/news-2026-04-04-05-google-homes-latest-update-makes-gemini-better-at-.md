## What Happened

Google is rolling out an update for its smart home app, Home, which aims to make controlling your smart home with its AI assistant, Gemini, more natural and reliable. The update introduces a new feature called "temperature control" that allows you to set the lighting color of your home according to descriptions.

"With this update, you can describe the color of the ocean, for example, and Gemini will adjust the lighting accordingly," the release notes state.

This update is significant because it expands the capabilities of the Home and Gemini ecosystem. By allowing users to control the lighting with such precision, it opens up new possibilities for personalized home automation.

## Why It Matters

This update is a major milestone for Google and the home automation industry. It further expands the reach and functionality of the Home ecosystem, providing users with more control over their homes. This feature also has the potential to enhance the overall user experience by making it easier for people to create a more personalized and efficient smart home environment.

## Context & Background

Google Home is a popular smart home device that uses the Assistant voice assistant to control various smart home devices. Gemini is the latest AI assistant from Google, which is designed to be more natural and conversational than its predecessor, Google Assistant.

The release of this update comes as Google is facing increased competition from other smart home manufacturers. To stay ahead of the curve, Google is constantly working to improve the Home ecosystem and introduce new features.

## What to Watch Next

The release of this update is a significant step in the evolution of smart home technology. It is expected to have a major impact on the way people interact with their homes, and it will be interesting to see how it evolves in the future.
