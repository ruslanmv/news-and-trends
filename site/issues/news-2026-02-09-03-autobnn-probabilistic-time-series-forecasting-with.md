## What Happened

AutoBNN, a generative neural network, has achieved a breakthrough in probabilistic time series forecasting. This breakthrough utilizes compositional bayesian neural networks to create highly accurate forecasts for various time series data.

The AutoBNN model utilizes a novel approach to generative modeling that goes beyond traditional recurrent neural networks. Instead of focusing on the sequential nature of time series, the AutoBNN model considers the underlying structure of the data through a set of latent variables. This approach allows the model to capture complex dependencies and relationships between different time series.

The results of the AutoBNN model demonstrate significant improvement in forecasting accuracy compared to traditional recurrent neural networks. The model achieved an RMSE of 0.01 for the stock market and 0.05 for the weather data.
