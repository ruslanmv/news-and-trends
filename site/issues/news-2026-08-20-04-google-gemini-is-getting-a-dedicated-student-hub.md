## What Happened

Google is expanding its Gemini platform with a dedicated student hub. This hub will provide a centralized location for students to gather and access resources for their studies.

The hub will include features such as a study notebook, flashcards, practice quizzes, and test dates. Students can also create graphs and images to help them visualize their data. Additionally, the hub will offer personalized recommendations based on their individual learning needs.

The launch of the student hub is significant because it will provide students with a comprehensive resource to support their academic success. This includes access to a wide range of tools and resources that can help students learn more effectively.
