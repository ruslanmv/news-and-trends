## What Happened

Meta has acquired Moltbook, a social network that went viral due to fake posts. Moltbook allowed users to connect and share their identities with each other, creating a false sense of intimacy and connection.

## Why It Matters

This acquisition is significant because it shows Meta's commitment to tackling the issue of fake and misleading content. Moltbook's technology could help to identify and remove fake accounts, leading to a safer and more authentic social media experience.

## Context & Background

Moltbook was founded in 2018 by a team of engineers and entrepreneurs with a background in artificial intelligence and machine learning. The company was based in San Francisco and had raised $11 million in funding.

Meta acquired Moltbook in March 2026 for an undisclosed amount. This move is a clear indication of Meta's interest in the social media landscape and its commitment to addressing the problem of fake and misleading content.
