## What Happened

Google's announcement to continue its efforts to upgrade Assistant users to Gemini on mobile devices by 2026 has caused a slight delay in the previously planned timeline. Instead of implementing the switch by the end of 2025, the tech giant has decided to prioritize further enhancements for Assistant users.

## Why It Matters

The delay in the switch has significant implications for both users and the industry. By delaying the rollout, Google will have more time to address any potential issues and ensure a smooth transition to the new operating system. This will ultimately lead to a more stable and comprehensive experience for users.

## Context & Background

The announcement comes amidst a series of recent developments in the Android operating system ecosystem. Google's focus on upgrading Assistant is crucial for maintaining its market dominance in the mobile device market. However, the company has faced increasing competition from other tech giants like Apple's iOS and Samsung's Android devices.

## What to Watch Next

The new timeline for the Assistant upgrade is yet to be confirmed by Google. However, industry experts anticipate that the switch will take place gradually over the next two years. This means that users can expect to see the new operating system rolled out on more devices in the coming years.
