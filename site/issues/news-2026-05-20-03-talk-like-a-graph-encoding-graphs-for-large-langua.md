## What Happened

Google unveiled a new tool called "Talking Graphs" at its annual AI summit on March 12, 2024. The tool allows users to interact with large language models (LLMs) by exploring and discussing their responses in a graph format. This innovation can significantly enhance the user experience and facilitate the exploration of complex topics and ideas.

## Why It Matters

The "Talking Graphs" feature is a game-changer for several reasons:

* **Enhanced user engagement:** By interacting with the LLM through a visual graph, users can engage with the AI in a more intuitive and engaging way.
* **Improved comprehension:** The graph visualization allows users to explore the relationships between concepts, making the AI's responses more comprehensive and easier to understand.
* **Expanded knowledge sharing:** Users can share and discuss ideas in a collaborative setting, fostering discussions and knowledge sharing across various domains.

## Context & Background

The announcement came alongside the release of the latest version of the LLM, "LaMDA 3," which boasts significantly enhanced capabilities and faster processing speed. This advancement paved the way for the development of "Talking Graphs," showcasing Google's ongoing commitment to pushing the boundaries of AI technology.

## What to Watch Next

The official release of "Talking Graphs" is expected to be available in the coming months. This innovative feature is poised to revolutionize the way people interact with and explore AI. As the technology matures and gains wider adoption, we can expect to see exciting applications across various industries, including education, healthcare, and business.
