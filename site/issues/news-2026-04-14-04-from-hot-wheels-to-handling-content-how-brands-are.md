## From Hot Wheels to Handling Content: How Brands Are Using Microsoft AI to Be More Productive and Imaginative

**What Happened:**

Microsoft's AI unit, "The Language Team," announced the development of a new language processing tool called "Creative AI." This tool uses AI to generate human-quality content, including scripts, stories, poems, and even code, based on specific prompts and instructions.

**Why It Matters:**

Creative AI holds immense potential to revolutionise content creation across various industries. By automating tedious tasks like writing and content generation, brands can save time and resources while potentially improving quality. This can lead to increased productivity, cost savings, and a boost in creativity.

**Context & Background:**

The AI industry is booming, with companies like Microsoft, Google, and Amazon investing heavily in research and development. This surge reflects the immense value that AI can bring to various sectors, including entertainment, marketing, and manufacturing.

**What to Watch Next:**

The launch of Creative AI is a major milestone for Microsoft's AI ambitions. The tool is currently in beta testing and is expected to be available to the public in the first quarter of 2023. This means that businesses and individuals can start exploring its capabilities and potential applications.
