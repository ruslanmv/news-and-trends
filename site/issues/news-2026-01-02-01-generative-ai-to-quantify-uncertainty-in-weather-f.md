## What Happened

The Google AI Blog announced the release of a new tool called Generative AI that can quantify uncertainty in weather forecasting. This tool uses machine learning to analyze historical data and predict the accuracy of weather forecasts. By identifying patterns and trends in weather patterns, Generative AI can identify areas with higher or lower probabilities of extreme weather events.

Generative AI has the potential to revolutionize weather forecasting by providing more accurate and reliable forecasts. This is important because extreme weather events can have a devastating impact on people and property. For example, a single hurricane can cause billions of dollars in damage and loss of life. By identifying areas where extreme weather events are more likely to occur, Generative AI can help to make communities more prepared for these events.
