## What Happened

ChatGPT Wallet is a new service from OpenAI that allows users to make purchases for their OpenAI agents. The wallet is currently available for ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US aged 18 and older using a US billing address and eligible US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa cards.

After adding a card to the wallet, users can use their agent to make purchases, such as movies, books, or even software. The service is expected to be available to all ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US by the end of 2026.

## Why It Matters

ChatGPT Wallet is a significant development for the chat AI industry. By allowing users to interact with their agents through purchases, ChatGPT is opening up a new avenue for engagement and monetization. This service will also help to further expand OpenAI's reach and influence.
