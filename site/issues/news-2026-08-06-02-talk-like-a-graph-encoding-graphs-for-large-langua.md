## What Happened

Google revealed a new AI project focused on encoding graphs for large language models. This project aims to address the challenge of how to represent and process relationships between entities within a graph. 

The goal is to create a richer and more nuanced understanding of natural language processing (NLP), which is the ability of computers to understand and generate human language. 

This new technology has potential applications in various fields, such as: 
- Natural language understanding (NLU) 
- Semantic search 
- Drug discovery 
- Sentiment analysis
