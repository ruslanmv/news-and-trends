## What Happened

Meta's Senior News Analyst, Sandhya Devanathan, has left the company to assume a new role at OpenAI. This news comes as Meta faces increasing scrutiny in India due to its data collection practices and handling of user data.

Devanathan's departure signifies the evolving role that Meta is playing in the Indian market. With her new position, she will oversee operations across Southeast Asia and Australia for OpenAI, a company known for its AI-powered products and services.

## Why It Matters

The news underscores Meta's commitment to expanding its reach and presence in the Indian market. By bringing Devanathan onboard, Meta aims to build a stronger presence in the region and capitalize on the growing AI ecosystem. This move also reflects Meta's recognition of the sensitivity surrounding data privacy and the need to comply with local regulations.

## Context & Background

Meta has been under increasing scrutiny in India due to its data collection practices and handling of user data. The Indian government has expressed concerns about Meta's use of data, including its use in targeted advertising and its sharing with other companies.

Meta has faced several legal and regulatory challenges regarding its data practices in India. In 2022, the Indian government fined Meta 1.7 billion rupees for allegedly violating data privacy laws.

## What to Watch Next

The development of Meta's data practices and its response to the Indian government's concerns will be closely watched by industry analysts and experts. It will also shed light on Meta's commitment to the Indian market and its ability to navigate the complex legal and regulatory landscape.
