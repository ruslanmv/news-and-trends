## What Happened

Google has launched new features for its AI platform, Gemini, allowing users to import another AI’s memory and chat history into their own Gemini account. This feature is designed to make it easier for users to transfer their existing AI knowledge and skills to a new platform or device.

The new "Import Memory" and "Import Chat History" features allow users to copy over everything their current AI already knows about them, including their preferences, favorite content, and conversations. This can be helpful for users who are migrating their AI to a different device or who want to share their AI with others.
