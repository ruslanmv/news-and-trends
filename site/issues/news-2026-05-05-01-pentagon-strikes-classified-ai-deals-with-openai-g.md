## What Happened

The Pentagon has struck deals with several major tech companies to allow for the use of their AI tools in classified settings. This news highlights the increasing importance of AI in defense and the potential it holds for national security.

## Why It Matters

The deals with OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Nvidia, Elon Musk's xAI, and the startup Reflection will allow the U.S. military to leverage AI technology in a secure and controlled manner. This collaboration is expected to significantly enhance the capabilities of the military's AI systems and contribute to the advancement of AI in defense.

## Context & Background

The Pentagon's decision to strike deals with these companies marks a significant shift in how AI is used in classified settings. In the past, Anthropic was the only major tech company with access to classified AI, but its relationship with the Pentagon has been severed due to concerns about privacy and ethical use of AI. These new deals with major tech companies signify a broader acceptance of AI in defense.

## What to Watch Next

The implementation of these agreements is expected to take several months, but it will have a significant impact on the military's AI capabilities. The collaboration between the Pentagon and these tech companies will allow for the development of new AI systems that can be used for classified missions. Additionally, it will help to ensure the ethical and responsible use of AI in defense.
