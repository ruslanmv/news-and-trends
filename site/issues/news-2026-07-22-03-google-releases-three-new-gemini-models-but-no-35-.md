## What Happened

Google unveiled the release of three new Gemini models – 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and Flash Cyber – at their annual developer conference. While each model boasts improved performance and versatility, the absence of a 3.5 Pro model casts a shadow on the announcement.

## Why It Matters

The absence of a 3.5 Pro signifies a significant shift in Google's AI strategy. With the 3.5 Pro being a powerful machine learning platform, the absence of a successor suggests a potential decrease in the use of 3.5 Pro for specific AI projects. This could lead to a wider range of models catering to diverse applications.

## Context & Background

The announcement comes amidst a wave of industry consolidation. As big tech companies face increasing competition, they are exploring ways to optimize their AI products. This release could be seen as a strategic move to retain market dominance and maintain profitability in the AI landscape.

## What to Watch Next

The release of the new Gemini models has sparked excitement within the AI community. With the absence of a 3.5 Pro, there are high expectations for the performance and capabilities of the 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and Flash Cyber. The next few years will likely witness intensive research and development to ensure these models are a success.
