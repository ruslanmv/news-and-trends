## What Happened

Generative AI has taken a significant step in weather forecasting by quantifying uncertainty in weather predictions. Researchers at Google have developed an algorithm that can analyze vast amounts of data and identify patterns that can help to improve weather forecasting.

The algorithm, called Generative Adversarial Networks, works by training two artificial intelligence models against each other. The first model, called the generator, is responsible for creating realistic weather patterns. The second model, called the discriminator, is responsible for identifying the true weather patterns from the generator's output.

The generator was able to create highly accurate weather patterns that matched real-world measurements with an error of less than 1%. This is a significant improvement over previous AI weather forecasting methods, which were often inaccurate and could lead to false weather forecasts.
