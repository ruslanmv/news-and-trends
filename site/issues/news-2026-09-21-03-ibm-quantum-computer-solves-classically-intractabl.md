## What Happened

IBM and University of Chicago researchers have achieved a groundbreaking milestone in quantum computing by solving a problem that was previously considered intractable. The system, which used a mere 70 error-corrected logical qubits, completed the task in a mere 15 minutes – significantly faster than classical methods that take hours or even days to achieve the same result.

## Why It Matters

This breakthrough holds immense significance in the field of quantum computing and cryptography. By solving a problem that was previously deemed unsolvable, the researchers have paved the way for new and more powerful quantum computers. This advancement has the potential to revolutionize various industries, including finance, healthcare, and materials science.

## Context & Background

The news is particularly relevant in the context of ongoing efforts to develop more powerful quantum computers. Quantum computers offer immense potential to tackle complex problems that are impossible for classical computers to solve. However, the technology is still in its early stages of development, and the 15-minute result is a significant milestone in demonstrating the feasibility of quantum computation.

## What to Watch Next

The scientific community is eagerly watching the progress of quantum computing research, with many experts predicting that the technology will continue to make significant strides. It is expected that the next major milestone will come from the development of larger and more efficient quantum computers. This would allow for further exploration of complex problems and could lead to breakthroughs in various industries.
