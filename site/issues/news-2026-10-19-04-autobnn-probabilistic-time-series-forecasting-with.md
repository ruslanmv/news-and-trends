## What Happened

AutoBNN is a new open-source library for probabilistic time series forecasting with compositional Bayesian neural networks. This powerful library allows researchers and practitioners to build and evaluate diverse probabilistic time series forecasting models directly within TensorFlow. This means that researchers can train, evaluate, and compare different models without needing to switch between different libraries.

The library introduces several innovations, including support for multiple loss functions, customized activation functions, and hierarchical Dirichlet process priors. This allows users to build complex and accurate models that better capture the underlying dynamics of the data.

## Why It Matters

AutoBNN significantly expands the capabilities of probabilistic time series forecasting. This is achieved by introducing several new features that make it easier for users to build and evaluate models. This will lead to a more efficient and accurate way to forecast time series data.

## Context & Background

AutoBNN is a relatively new library, first released in 2023. However, it has quickly gained popularity in the machine learning and time series forecasting communities. This is due to its ease of use, its wide range of features, and its ability to produce high-quality forecasts.

AutoBNN is currently under active development by a team of researchers at Google AI. The library is actively being extended with new features and improvements.

## What to Watch Next

Researchers are actively working on improving the performance of AutoBNN. The latest version of the library includes several new features, such as support for multiple loss functions and hierarchical Dirichlet process priors. These features are expected to significantly improve the accuracy and efficiency of time series forecasting.
