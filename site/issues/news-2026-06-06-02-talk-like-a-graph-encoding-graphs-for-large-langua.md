## What Happened

Google has unveiled a new feature for its AI platform, Graphcore, which allows users to encode and access graphs directly within their natural language queries. This feature, named "Talk Like a Graph," enables users to communicate their ideas and intentions through the understanding of graph relationships and connections.

The ability to encode and access graphs within queries offers several advantages. For instance, users can express complex ideas using natural language, which can then be processed by the AI model. This facilitates a more intuitive and efficient way for users to interact with the AI.

## Why It Matters

This groundbreaking feature has the potential to revolutionize the way users interact with AI. By enabling users to express their ideas through graphs, it can lead to a more intuitive and engaging user experience. This can improve the quality of the AI's responses and make it more relevant to user needs.

## Context & Background

Graphcore is a proprietary technology developed by Google that allows users to represent and query relationships between entities. This technology has the potential to unlock significant new possibilities for AI applications, such as natural language processing, question answering, and knowledge discovery.

In recent years, there has been a growing interest in the use of graph data in AI. Graph data can provide richer and more contextually rich representations of relationships between entities compared to traditional text-based data. This can lead to more accurate and efficient AI models.

## What to Watch Next

Google plans to begin rolling out Talk Like a Graph in the coming months, with the feature initially available to a limited group of users. The company has also announced plans to collaborate with various industry partners to develop and implement this technology.

The success of Talk Like a Graph remains to be seen. However, if it proves successful, it could have a major impact on the way AI is used in various industries, including healthcare, finance, and marketing.
