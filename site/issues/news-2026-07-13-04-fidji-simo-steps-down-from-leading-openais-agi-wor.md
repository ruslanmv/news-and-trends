## What Happened

Fidji Simo, the Chief of OpenAI's AGI (artificial intelligence) division, is stepping down from her full-time role due to illness. Simo made the announcement on X, with a brief explanation of her absence.

## Why It Matters

This decision comes as a surprise, as Simo had been a driving force in OpenAI's AGI efforts since joining the company in 2019. Her departure is expected to have a significant impact on the company's AI endeavors.

## Context & Background

Simo's departure comes as OpenAI faces a challenging landscape of rapid technological advancements and increasing competition. The company's AGI projects have shown promise, but they have also faced criticism for being opaque and slow to iterate. Simo's departure will likely further challenge OpenAI's already-complex decision-making process.

## What to Watch Next

The future of OpenAI's AGI division remains uncertain. The company has yet to announce any immediate plans for filling her position. However, rumors suggest that Simo may take a break from the tech industry before returning to a full-time role.
