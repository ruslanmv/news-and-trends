## What Happened

Anthropic has launched a new feature that allows users to interact with interactive apps directly within the chatbot interface. This means users can call up relevant apps like Slack and other workplace tools directly from the chatbot, streamlining communication and boosting productivity.

## Why It Matters

This feature is significant as it enhances the user experience by offering a more intuitive and efficient way to access essential tools and information. This feature also creates opportunities for increased collaboration and knowledge sharing within teams.

## Context & Background

The new interactive app feature is a significant milestone for Anthropic, expanding its reach and capabilities. It also aligns with the company's commitment to providing innovative solutions that streamline processes and improve team collaboration.

## What to Watch Next

The release of interactive apps within the chatbot interface is expected to be a major success, with a wide range of apps and tools expected to be added in the future. Additionally, Anthropic is working on integrating Cowork into the app, further enhancing collaboration within teams.
