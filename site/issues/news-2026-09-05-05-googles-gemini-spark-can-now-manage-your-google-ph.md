## What Happened

Google's Gemini Spark can now manage your Google Photos library, a new feature that allows users to access and interact with their photos through an AI-powered interface. With Gemini Spark, users can:

* Edit and curate photo albums
* Create shared collections
* Turn photos into calendar events
* Manage other photo-related tasks

This feature is available to Google Photos Pro and Ultra subscribers, and it offers a convenient way for users to streamline their photo management and share them with others.

## Why It Matters

The ability to manage your photos through an AI interface offers several benefits, including:

* **Time savings:** Users can easily edit, curate, and share their photos without having to manually navigate through the Google Photos website or app.
* **Enhanced organization:** The feature helps users to organize and categorize their photos based on various criteria, making it easier to find what they are looking for.
* **Improved collaboration:** Shared collections allow users to collaborate with others on photo projects and share them with a wider audience.
* **Increased privacy:** Users can control who can see their photos and share them with specific audiences.
