## What Happened

OpenAI has introduced a new feature that allows users to directly adjust ChatGPT's enthusiasm level. This new feature gives users more control over how ChatGPT interacts and provides them with additional flexibility when working with the AI language model.

## Why It Matters

This feature is significant because it gives users more control over ChatGPT's tone and personality. This can be beneficial for a variety of tasks, such as writing more engaging content, providing more focused information, or fostering more collaborative brainstorming sessions. It can also be used to address specific challenges, such as generating more persuasive or creative content.

## Context & Background

OpenAI is a rapidly evolving AI language model that is capable of producing a wide range of creative and informative content. However, the default setting of ChatGPT can be quite formal and objective. This new feature allows users to adjust ChatGPT's tone and personality to a more conversational and engaging style.

## What to Watch Next

The introduction of this new feature is a major milestone for OpenAI. This feature is likely to have a significant impact on how users interact with ChatGPT and how they use the AI language model for various tasks.
