## What Happened

Google’s AI unit, DeepMind, unveiled a new technique called “Graphene Language Modeling” at their annual AI summit. This breakthrough leverages the power of symbolic graph data to enhance the efficiency of language models.

**Key Facts:**

- Graphene, a single layer of atoms arranged in a hexagonal lattice, holds immense potential for data representation.

- This new method utilizes the unique properties of graphene to enable faster and more accurate language modeling.

- The technique can handle complex natural language tasks like text generation, sentiment analysis, and machine translation.
