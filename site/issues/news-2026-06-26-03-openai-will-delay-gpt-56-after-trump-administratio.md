## What Happened

The Trump administration has requested OpenAI to delay the release of its next big-ticket model, GPT-5.6. GPT-5.6 is expected to be a massive model with the potential to revolutionize AI technology. However, due to concerns about potential security risks, the administration is reportedly hesitant to release it to the public.

## Why It Matters

GPT-5.6 has the potential to be a game-changer in the AI industry. It is expected to be much more advanced than its predecessor, GPT-5.5, and could perform a wide range of tasks, such as language translation, text generation, and image creation.

## Context & Background

GPT-5.6 is a large language model, a type of AI that has been trained on a massive dataset of text and code. These models have the potential to learn and adapt like humans, and they are also able to generate human-quality text.

Due to concerns about potential misuse, the development and release of GPT-5.6 has been highly restricted. OpenAI has been working closely with the government to ensure that the model is used in a responsible manner.

## What to Watch Next

The release of GPT-5.6 is still a matter of speculation. However, it is expected to happen in the next few years. OpenAI has said that it will release GPT-5.6 in limited preview form to a small group of employees and partners. They plan to release it to the public in the second half of 2026.
