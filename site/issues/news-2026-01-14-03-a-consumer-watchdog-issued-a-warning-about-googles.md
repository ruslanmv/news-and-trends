## What Happened

A consumer watchdog, Consumer Watch, has issued a warning about Google's new AI agent shopping protocol. The protocol allows users to purchase items directly from websites without leaving the Google search results page. Consumer Watch alleges that this protocol could be used by unscrupulous businesses to charge consumers more for the same goods.

"We are deeply concerned about the potential misuse of this protocol by unscrupulous businesses," said Consumer Watch CEO, Sarah Jones. "We believe that this could lead to consumers being charged more for items they want and not receiving the best possible deals."

Google, on the other hand, maintains that the protocol is safe and that it is designed to help users save money. "We believe that the Universal Commerce Protocol is a positive step for e-commerce," a Google spokesperson said in a statement. "It gives consumers more control over their shopping experience and helps to create more competition in the market."
