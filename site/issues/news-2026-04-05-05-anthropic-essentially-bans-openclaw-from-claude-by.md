## What Happened

Anthropic's OpenClaw service, which allows users to connect their Claude subscription with third-party AI applications, will soon be significantly more expensive. Beginning April 4th at 3PM ET, users will no longer be able to use their Claude subscription limits for third-party harnesses including OpenClaw. This decision was made due to Anthropic's desire to ensure the stability and security of its platform.

## Why It Matters

For users, this change could mean a significant increase in subscription costs, potentially affecting their financial plans. For Claude, it could limit the availability of their AI applications and features.

## Context & Background

OpenClaw has been a popular integration for Claude users, allowing them to access a wide range of AI applications at no additional cost. However, Anthropic's decision reflects the growing market demand for privacy and data security. As AI technology advances, protecting user data and ensuring the responsible use of AI applications has become increasingly important.

## What to Watch Next

The new pricing structure will be effective immediately, with users receiving a notification email outlining the changes. While this may raise concerns about the future of OpenClaw integration, it also demonstrates Anthropic's commitment to maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and security.
