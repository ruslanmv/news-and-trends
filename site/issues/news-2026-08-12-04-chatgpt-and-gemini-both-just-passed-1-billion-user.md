## What Happened

The Verge reported that ChatGPT and Gemini have both reached a massive milestone by reaching 1 billion users. This achievement comes 14 consecutive times for ChatGPT and is the fastest-growing product for Google.

With this billion-user milestone, both ChatGPT and Gemini are experiencing tremendous growth, with ChatGPT experiencing a 33% increase in monthly usage and Gemini's user base increasing by 28%.

The news underscores the growing popularity of AI applications like ChatGPT and the potential for such technologies to have a significant impact on society and the economy.
