## What Happened
Google has announced the release of a new feature for its AI language model, Large Language Model (LLM). This feature allows users to encode and decode graphs with their LLM, enabling them to interact with the model in a more visual and interactive way.

## Why It Matters
This new feature has the potential to revolutionize the way users interact with LLM by providing a more intuitive and user-friendly experience. By enabling users to visualize and interact with the model, Google aims to make it easier for them to learn, perform tasks, and solve problems.

## Context & Background
The announcement comes at a time when LLMs are increasingly being used in various industries, including finance, healthcare, and customer service. As LLMs become more powerful and accessible, it's important to develop methods that allow users to interact with them in a more natural and intuitive way.

## What to Watch Next
The release of this new feature is a significant milestone in the development of AI, and it's likely that we will see more features and improvements to LLM in the future. In particular, it would be interesting to see how this feature could be used in educational settings to help students learn about AI and programming.
