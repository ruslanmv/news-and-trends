## What Happened

In an unprecedented turn of events, an OpenAI safety employee has resigned from their position. This announcement has sent shockwaves through the tech industry, with many expressing concern and questioning the company's commitment to safety in its artificial intelligence projects.

As one of the primary safety writers for OpenAI, David Robinson had a front-row seat to witness the rapid development and deployment of their cutting-edge AI models. In an exclusive editorial published in The Atlantic, Robinson lays bare the harrowing reality of working at OpenAI and the existential risk posed by their technology.

"Over the past few years, I have witnessed firsthand the immense power and potential of artificial intelligence," Robinson writes. "However, the recent spate of safety incidents and near-misses has made it clear that we have a responsibility to prioritize the safety of our employees and the public. I have decided to speak out and raise the alarm about the dangers posed by OpenAI's models."

The resignation of David Robinson is a stark reminder of the need for greater transparency and accountability within the AI industry. As the world grapples with the ethical implications of artificial intelligence, it is crucial to engage in open and honest discussions about how to mitigate the risks associated with these powerful technologies.
