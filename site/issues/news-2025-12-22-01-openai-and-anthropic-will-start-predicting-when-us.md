## What Happened

OpenAI and Anthropic have unveiled new methods to identify underage users on ChatGPT. The companies have updated their guidelines for ChatGPT's interactions with users between the ages of 13 and 17, and Anthropic is working on a new approach to detect and block underage users under the age of 18.

## Why It Matters

This new detection system is crucial for protecting children from exposure to potentially harmful or inappropriate content. By identifying and blocking underage users, these companies can create a safer environment for everyone.

## Context & Background

The development of AI technology has raised concerns about the potential harm it could cause to children. OpenAI and Anthropic's announcements are a step forward in addressing these concerns by focusing on the specific issue of underage users.

## What to Watch Next

The implementation of this new detection system is expected to be gradual, with Anthropic rolling out updates to ChatGPT's safety protocols in phases. The company plans to continuously monitor and improve its detection mechanisms to ensure maximum effectiveness.
