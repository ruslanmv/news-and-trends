## What Happened

OpenAI and Anthropic have teamed up to implement new measures to detect underage users. ChatGPT's guidelines have been updated to include safeguards for users between the ages of 13 and 17, prompting Anthropic to develop a new algorithm for identifying and blocking underage users.

## Why It Matters

This collaboration signifies a significant step forward in safeguarding children's online safety. By implementing proactive measures like these, OpenAI and Anthropic aim to prevent underage users from engaging in inappropriate and harmful activities on their platforms.

## Context & Background

The news comes amidst ongoing discussions surrounding the safety of AI and the potential impact on vulnerable demographics. The evolving conversation underscores the need for robust safeguards to protect children from the dangers of AI.

## What to Watch Next

The release of this announcement is the first step in a broader initiative to refine the detection and prevention of underage use on these platforms. OpenAI and Anthropic plan to continuously monitor and update their algorithms to stay ahead of evolving trends and challenges.
