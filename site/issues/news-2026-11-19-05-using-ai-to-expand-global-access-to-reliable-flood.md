## Using AI to Expand Global Access to Reliable Flood Forecasts

**What Happened**

Google's AI unit, DeepMind, has partnered with the World Food Programme (WFP) to develop an AI-powered flood forecasting system that could help improve access to timely and reliable flood forecasts in underserved communities worldwide. The system, called "Flood Forecast Expansion," uses machine learning algorithms to analyze historical weather data and predict flood risks. By leveraging AI, the system aims to enhance the accuracy and accessibility of flood forecasts, particularly in regions that are often overlooked by traditional forecasting methods.

**Why It Matters**

Flood forecasting is a critical issue in many parts of the world, with devastating consequences for human health and infrastructure. Traditional weather forecasting methods often struggle to accurately predict flood risks, particularly in remote or poorly monitored areas. This is due to a number of factors, including limited access to data, poor weather station network, and the complexity of flood formation.

The Flood Forecast Expansion project hopes to address these challenges by leveraging AI to improve the accuracy and accessibility of flood forecasts. By doing so, the project has the potential to save lives and livelihoods, particularly in regions that are most vulnerable to floods.

**Context & Background**

The project is a collaboration between Google AI, the WFP, and the Global Food Security Monitoring and Assessment (GFSMA) network. The GFSMA network is a global partnership of institutions and organizations that work together to monitor and assess food security in developing countries.

The need for improved flood forecasting is particularly acute in regions that are already experiencing climate change. Climate change is expected to lead to more frequent and severe floods, which could strain water resources and infrastructure systems. By providing improved flood forecasts, the Flood Forecast Expansion project can help to mitigate these risks and ensure that people are better prepared for floods.

**What to Watch Next**

The Flood Forecast Expansion project is expected to launch in the early 2020s. The system will be tested in a limited number of countries before being rolled out to the entire WFP network. The project's success will depend on a number of factors, including the quality of the AI models used and the availability of data. However, if successful, the Flood Forecast Expansion project could have a significant impact on flood forecasting in developing countries.
