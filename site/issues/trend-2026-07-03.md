* **Rise of Conversational Assistants:** The use of conversational AI assistants will continue to grow, with devices such as Google Assistant and Alexa offering more personalized and convenient experiences.

* **Expansion into Other Domains:** Conversational AI will find applications in various fields, including healthcare, education, and customer support.
