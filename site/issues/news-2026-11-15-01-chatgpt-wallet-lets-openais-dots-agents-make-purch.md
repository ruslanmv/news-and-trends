## What Happened

ChatGPT Wallet is a new wallet that lets OpenAI's Dots AI agents make purchases for you. This service is available to ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US aged 18 and older using a US billing address and "eligible US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa cards."

After adding a card to the wallet, you can instruct your agent to perform various tasks, such as shopping, paying bills, or managing your finances. The wallet is currently available for a limited time in the US and will soon be available worldwide.

## Why It Matters

This new wallet is a game-changer for users of OpenAI's Dots AI agents. It allows users to leverage the computing power of these AI agents without needing to set up or manage a separate device or account. This could lead to a more streamlined and efficient way to use AI for tasks such as shopping, investing, and managing finances.

## Context & Background

The launch of ChatGPT Wallet is part of OpenAI's ongoing expansion into the financial sector. The company has already launched ChatBook, a platform that connects users with financial professionals, and is developing ChatInvest, a service that allows users to invest in stocks and bonds through AI.

ChatGPT Wallet is a significant milestone in OpenAI's journey to become a major player in the financial services industry. The company has the resources and expertise to make this a successful venture, and the launch of ChatGPT Wallet is a testament to their commitment to innovation.

## What to Watch Next

The next major milestone in the development of ChatGPT Wallet is the launch of the wallet in other countries. The company has already received approval for the wallet in the UK and Germany, and is working to expand to other countries in the coming months.

The success of ChatGPT Wallet will have a significant impact on the financial services industry. By making it easier for users to interact with AI, ChatGPT Wallet will drive adoption of AI in a new area of the financial industry.
