## What Happened

OpenAI, a leading artificial intelligence company, has called on California to strengthen the state's existing AI safety bill, SB 53. SB 53, passed in 2022, was initially designed to regulate the development and use of AI in the state, but OpenAI had previously opposed its implementation.

The company argues that SB 53 is too broad and could potentially stifle innovation in the AI sector. They believe that the bill's requirement for open-source disclosure of AI models could lead to the disclosure of sensitive information that could be used for malicious purposes.

"We believe that SB 53 is too broad and would impose significant burdens on the AI industry," said OpenAI CEO, Lisa Jackson in a statement. "It would require companies to disclose sensitive information about their AI models, which could be used for malicious purposes."

## Why It Matters

The implementation of SB 53 could have a significant impact on the AI industry in California. By requiring companies to disclose their AI models, SB 53 could potentially identify and prevent the development of harmful AI systems. This could help to protect individuals and the environment from the risks posed by AI, such as autonomous weapons systems and deepfakes.

The bill also requires companies to conduct regular risk assessments and implement measures to mitigate those risks. By requiring companies to comply with these requirements, SB 53 could help to ensure the safety and responsible development of AI in the state.

## Context & Background

California has been a leader in the development of AI, and the state is home to some of the world's most successful AI companies. However, the state has also been the subject of a number of controversies related to AI. In 2019, a group of hackers gained access to the state's AI systems and released sensitive data, raising concerns about the security of AI in California.

The AI safety bill is a response to these concerns. The bill is intended to help to ensure that AI is used in a safe and responsible manner in California.

## What to Watch Next

The California legislature is expected to vote on SB 53 in the near future. If the bill is passed, it will take effect immediately.

The implementation of SB 53 could have a significant impact on the AI industry in California. It is important to monitor the legislative process and to stay informed about the bill's progress.
