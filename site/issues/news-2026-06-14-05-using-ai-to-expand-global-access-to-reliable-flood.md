## Using AI to Expand Global Access to Reliable Flood Forecasts

**What Happened**

Google's AI team unveiled a new solution called "FloodPredict" which uses artificial intelligence to expand the accessibility and accuracy of flood forecasts globally. The tool leverages machine learning techniques to analyze a vast amount of data including weather patterns, satellite imagery, and hydrological data to predict flood risk. By combining this information with local knowledge and weather observations, FloodPredict provides a comprehensive and real-time view of flood potential.

**Why It Matters**

Flood prediction is a critical issue that can save lives and property. Traditional flood forecasting methods often rely on expensive and limited data sources, leading to inaccurate predictions and missed alerts. By utilizing AI, FloodPredict offers a more reliable and accessible approach to flood forecasting. This improved accuracy can lead to timely evacuation efforts and increased preparedness to mitigate the impact of floods.

**Context & Background**

The increasing frequency and severity of extreme weather events, coupled with climate change, have put a strain on traditional flood prediction systems. Traditional methods often struggle to keep pace with these changes, resulting in inaccurate forecasts and missed alerts.

**What to Watch Next**

FloodPredict is expected to launch in early 2024 and will initially be available for use by humanitarian and emergency agencies. The project is expected to have a significant impact on flood risk management and disaster preparedness around the world.
