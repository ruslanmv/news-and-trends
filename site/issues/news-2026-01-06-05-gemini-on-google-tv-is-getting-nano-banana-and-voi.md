## What Happened

The Verge reports that Gemini on Google TV is getting a major update that makes the AI assistant more helpful and more visually engaging. The update introduces Nano Banana and Veo support for creating AI videos and images directly on your TV. This means you can modify your family photos for a laugh or create stunning images for your home décor.

## Why It Matters

This update is significant because it adds a new dimension to the already impressive capabilities of the Gemini AI assistant. By allowing users to create AI videos and images directly on their TV, it opens up a whole new world of possibilities for entertainment and creativity. This is particularly beneficial for families with children, as it allows them to engage with AI in a fun and engaging way.
