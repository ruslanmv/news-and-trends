## What Happened

Meta's Muse AI is getting a major upgrade with the launch of Spaces. This new feature allows users to interact with their AI helpers in a more collaborative setting. Spaces is built on top of Meta's existing AI infrastructure, which means users can already access their Muse agents within the Spaces app.

## Why It Matters

Spaces is a major step for Meta in making its AI services more accessible and engaging. This feature will allow users to work with their Muse agents more easily and efficiently, which should lead to improved results and a more satisfying experience.

## Context & Background

Meta's Muse AI is a powerful tool for creating and managing chatbots and AI-powered virtual assistants. Spaces is the first app that integrates directly with Muse, allowing users to access their AI helpers within the app itself. This integration will make it easier for users to interact with their Muse agents and get the help they need.

## What to Watch Next

The official release date for Spaces is not yet announced. However, Meta has already confirmed that the app will be available on both iOS and Android devices. Users can expect to see a major update to their Muse app soon.
