## What Happened

Microsoft is planning to train its sales team members on how to talk down and potentially silence OpenAI and Anthropic, two powerful artificial intelligence (AI) language models. These models can generate human-quality text, and their ability to understand and respond to natural language has raised concerns about the ethical and societal implications of their use.

Microsoft's decision to take this approach reflects the growing concerns about these models. Microsoft has been closely monitoring the development of AI, and it has expressed its concern about the potential for these models to be used for malicious purposes.

In addition to training its sales team, Microsoft is also working to develop new tools and technologies to help its customers manage and control AI. These tools could include AI content moderation platforms that would identify and remove potentially harmful or misleading AI-generated content.

## Why It Matters

Microsoft's announcement is significant because it is a major player in the AI industry. By investing in training its sales team on how to talk down AI, Microsoft is sending a message that it is serious about protecting its customers from the potential dangers of AI.

This announcement could also have a major impact on the broader AI industry. By forcing Microsoft's sales team to understand the risks associated with AI, Microsoft could help to raise awareness and build trust among potential customers.

Microsoft's announcement comes at a time when AI is rapidly developing. The company has been a major player in the AI industry from its inception, and it has been at the forefront of developing new AI products and technologies.
