## Using AI to Expand Global Access to Reliable Flood Forecasts

**What Happened**

Google has launched a new AI-powered service called “Flood Forecast” that aims to expand global access to reliable flood forecasts by using machine learning to analyze and predict flood risks in real-time. The service is currently available in select countries and will be gradually expanded to more regions in the coming months.

**Why It Matters**

Flood forecasts are crucial for early flood warning systems, which can save lives and property. By providing accurate and timely flood forecasts, Flood Forecast can help communities around the world better prepare for and respond to floods. This is particularly important in regions that are prone to flooding, such as coastal areas and river basins.

**Context & Background**

The demand for flood forecasting services is increasing due to the growing population and urbanization in flood-prone regions. Traditional weather forecasting methods are often limited in their ability to provide accurate forecasts due to the complex nature of flood occurrence.

**What to Watch Next**

The launch of Flood Forecast is a significant step forward in improving global flood forecasting capabilities. The service is expected to have a major impact on flood risk management and disaster preparedness around the world. As the technology is further developed and expanded, it has the potential to provide invaluable insights into flood risk worldwide.
