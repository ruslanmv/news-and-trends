## What Happened

Anthropic has revealed a new approach to text watermarks that will be applied to Claude's generative AI. This system will comply with Europe's AI transparency rules and create detectable patterns using wording probabilities.

The announcement comes as Anthropic faces increasing scrutiny in the wake of an incident where its text watermarking technology was used to create a deepfake of a prominent political figure. This incident has raised concerns about the potential misuse of AI watermarks for malicious purposes.


## Why It Matters

This new system addresses these concerns by providing a transparent and verifiable way to watermark AI-generated content. This will allow users and regulators to track and detect fake or misleading AI-generated content, promoting transparency and trust in the AI ecosystem.

## Context & Background

The development of this watermarking system is significant as it demonstrates Anthropic's commitment to addressing the ethical and legal challenges associated with AI. The company has been actively researching and developing AI watermarking technology, and this announcement marks a major milestone in its efforts.

## What to Watch Next

The implementation of this new watermarking system is expected to be gradual, and Anthropic will provide updates on the timeline and milestones leading up to its official launch. The company also plans to work with partners and regulatory bodies to develop clear guidelines and best practices for the use of AI watermarks.
