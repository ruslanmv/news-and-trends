## What Happened

OpenAI has announced that they will begin testing ads in ChatGPT today. The ads will appear in a separate area beneath the chatbot, and OpenAI expects them to make up less than half of their revenue in the long term.

This move represents a significant step in AI advertising, as it marks the first time that large language models like ChatGPT have been used to create targeted ads.

## Why It Matters

This development has a number of important implications for the advertising industry. First, it opens up a new avenue for targeting consumers with highly personalized ads. Second, it raises concerns about the transparency and accountability of AI advertising. Finally, it could have a major impact on the future of content creation and distribution.

## Context & Background

OpenAI's announcement comes at a time when the AI advertising industry is rapidly growing. In 2022, the global AI advertising market was valued at $12.5 billion, and is expected to reach $25.4 billion by 2027.

ChatGPT is a large language model that has been trained on a massive amount of text data. It is capable of generating human-quality text, including ads.

The emergence of ChatGPT has raised concerns about its potential to be used for malicious purposes, such as spreading misinformation or generating fake news. OpenAI's decision to test ads in ChatGPT is a step to address these concerns.

## What to Watch Next

The first phase of ChatGPT's ad testing will likely involve small businesses and startups. However, it is possible that larger companies will follow suit. Additionally, the type of ads that are tested will likely vary depending on the specific use case.

It will be important to monitor the development of AI advertising and to be aware of the potential implications for society.
