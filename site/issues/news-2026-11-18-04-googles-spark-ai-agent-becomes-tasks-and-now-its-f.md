## What Happened

Google is rebranding its Spark AI agent as "Tasks" and making it free to use. The feature, which is powered by Google's new Gemini 4 Hydrogen AI model, can do things like create meal plans, manage your calendar, and dig through your emails to find recurring expenses you might not need. Tasks is designed to help users be more efficient and organized in their daily lives.

## Why It Matters

Tasks is a major upgrade to Google's Spark AI agent. This free version of the agent offers several benefits to users, including:

* **Improved productivity:** Tasks can help users save time by automating tasks and managing their schedule more effectively.
* **Reduced stress:** By eliminating the need to manually track and manage expenses, Tasks can help users reduce stress and anxiety.
* **Increased control:** Tasks gives users more control over their finances by allowing them to set spending limits and track their spending in real-time.
