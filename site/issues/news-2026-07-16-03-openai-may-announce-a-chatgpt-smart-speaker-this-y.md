## What Happened

OpenAI is preparing to announce a ChatGPT-powered smart speaker this year. This device will have no screen, but will utilize a camera and additional sensors to understand its environment and respond accordingly.

The company recently filed a lawsuit against Apple, alleging that Apple's recently launched HomePod competitor infringes on OpenAI's intellectual property. The lawsuit indicates OpenAI's commitment to protecting its technology, and could have significant implications for the future of AI-powered devices.

## Why It Matters

The launch of this smart speaker would be a major milestone for OpenAI and the AI industry. It would demonstrate the growing capabilities of AI and its potential to permeate everyday life. The device could also help to further the adoption of AI in the home, and provide users with a more intuitive way to interact with technology.

## Context & Background

The news of OpenAI's plans to release a smart speaker comes as the company faces a lawsuit from Apple over the HomePod. This lawsuit highlights the increasing competition in the AI market, and raises concerns about the future of AI development. OpenAI's smart speaker would be a key player in this market, and its success could have a significant impact on the industry.

## What to Watch Next

It is expected that OpenAI will announce the launch of its smart speaker sometime this year. The device is expected to be priced competitively with the Apple HomePod, and could have a wide range of features. Some of the features that have been rumored to be included include:

- A full-color camera
- A voice assistant with natural language processing
- Voice control over smart home devices
- Personalized learning and skills
