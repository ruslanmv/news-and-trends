## What Happened
OpenAI has just joined the growing list of companies building their own chips, signaling a challenge to Nvidia's dominance in the AI chip market. The company will release its custom inference chip called Jalapeno, built with Broadcom, which will compete with Nvidia's existing offerings like the DGX and Tesla V100.

## Why It Matters
OpenAI's Jalapeno chip is a significant milestone in the AI chip industry. It demonstrates the growing trend of companies diversifying their supply chains by building their own chips, reducing the risk of being dependent on single suppliers. This could lead to increased competition and lower prices for AI chips.

## Context & Background
OpenAI's announcement comes at a time when Nvidia faces increasing competition from competitors like Google, Apple, and SpaceX. The company's dominance in the AI chip market has been remarkable, but it faces increasing pressure from these rivals. This new chip from OpenAI could help them stay competitive and maintain their market share.

## What to Watch Next
The development of OpenAI's Jalapeno chip is expected to accelerate in the coming years. The company plans to begin shipping the chip in the second half of 2026, with mass production starting in 2027. This will likely put pressure on Nvidia to develop new features and partnerships to stay ahead of the competition.
