## What Happened

Google's AI team unveiled a new approach to natural language processing (NLP) called GraphLM, which can generate human-quality text by learning from the structure of information in real-world connections and relationships.

GraphLM is a neural network that encodes information into a graph, where nodes represent entities and edges represent relationships between them. This allows the model to learn the meaning of text by observing how different pieces of information are connected.

The model was trained on a massive dataset of text and code, and it was able to generate human-quality text that was similar to the training data. The team also showed that GraphLM could learn to generate text in multiple languages.

This new approach has the potential to revolutionize the way we create content and understand the world around us. By learning from the structure of information, GraphLM can generate text that is more accurate and creative than traditional NLP methods.
