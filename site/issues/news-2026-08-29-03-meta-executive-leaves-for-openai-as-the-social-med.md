## What Happened
Sandhya Devanathan, the current head of Meta's AI operations in Southeast Asia and Australia, will be leaving the company to take on a new role at OpenAI. The news comes as Meta faces increasing scrutiny and criticism in India due to its data collection practices and ethical concerns.

## Why It Matters
The departure of Sandhya Devanathan signifies a significant shift for Meta and could have a broader impact on the Indian tech landscape. Her expertise and leadership in AI will be valuable assets to OpenAI, a company known for its commitment to responsible AI development.

## Context & Background
Meta's AI operations have come under intense scrutiny in India, with concerns around data privacy, transparency, and the use of facial recognition technology. The company has faced several lawsuits related to these issues, and its data collection practices have been widely criticized.

## What to Watch Next
Following Sandhya Devanathan's departure, OpenAI will likely appoint a new leader to oversee its AI operations in the region. This will be crucial to ensuring the company remains compliant with local regulations and addresses the concerns of the Indian government and citizens.
