## What Happened

Google's AI unit has developed a new technology that can assist healthcare professionals in lung cancer screening, potentially reducing the number of unnecessary tests and leading to more accurate diagnoses.

The system, called "LumenSight," uses AI and optical technology to analyze medical images of the lungs, identifying areas of potential abnormalities. This can help doctors make more informed decisions, reducing the need for invasive tests that can be uncomfortable and risky for patients.

The system is still in its early stages of development, but it has the potential to revolutionize lung cancer screening by reducing the number of unnecessary tests and leading to more accurate diagnoses. This could lead to improved patient outcomes and reduced healthcare costs.

## Why It Matters

Lung cancer is the leading cause of cancer death in the United States, with an estimated 251,550 new cases and 78,550 deaths in 2023. Early detection is crucial for improving the chances of successful treatment, and lung cancer screening tests are often used for this purpose.
