## What Happened

The Verge reported the news that Google has expanded its AI music-making capabilities to the Gemini app. This feature allows users to generate 30-second tracks based on text, images, and videos, without leaving the conversational chatbot window. The music-making tool, named Lyria 3, is powered by DeepMind's AI model and offers a unique way for users to create music with a personalized touch.

## Why It Matters

The news's significance lies in its potential to revolutionize the way people create music. By offering a convenient and accessible way to generate music, Lyria 3 can attract a wide range of users, from musicians and hobbyists to casual listeners. This innovative feature is expected to enhance the user experience and unlock new creative possibilities within the app.

## Context & Background

The news highlights the burgeoning field of AI and its impact on various industries. The article also emphasizes the importance of accessibility and democratization in the AI space, as tools like Lyria 3 can empower individuals with creative limitations to express themselves through music.

## What to Watch Next

With the official launch of Lyria 3, users can expect Google to actively incorporate this feature into the Gemini app. The official rollout is expected to take place in the coming weeks, with the app's developers keeping users informed through updates and announcements. This collaboration between Google and DeepMind signifies the growing influence of AI in the music industry.
