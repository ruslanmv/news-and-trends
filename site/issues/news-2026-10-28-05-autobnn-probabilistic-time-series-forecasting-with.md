## What Happened

AutoBNN is a novel probabilistic time series forecasting technique that utilizes compositional Bayesian neural networks (CBNNs) to generate and improve future time series predictions. This approach offers significant advantages over traditional time series models by addressing the limitations of pure CBNNs, which can suffer from high computational costs and limited generalization ability.

AutoBNN utilizes a novel approach called "compositional augmentation" to address these limitations. Compositional augmentation involves generating synthetic data by progressively adding and removing features from existing data, effectively constructing a library of diverse synthetic samples. This technique not only improves computational efficiency but also enhances the model's ability to learn complex relationships in the data.

The proposed method is particularly effective for time series with high dimensionality and complex dynamics. Simulations demonstrate its ability to achieve state-of-the-art forecasting accuracy on diverse datasets, including financial returns, weather patterns, and economic indicators.
