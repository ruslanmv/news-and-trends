## What Happened

The Verge announced the release of Lyria 3, a new AI music maker available on the Gemini app. This beta access allows users to generate 30-second tracks based on text, images, and videos, without leaving the chatbot window.

The new music-making tool is powered by DeepMind's latest audio model, and it promises to be a game-changer for music creation. With Lyria 3, users can explore a wide range of sounds and styles, from pop to hip-hop to ambient, all generated by AI.

## Why It Matters

Lyria 3 is a significant development in AI music generation, as it opens up new possibilities for creative expression and collaboration. By allowing users to create music without human intervention, Lyria 3 can streamline the songwriting process and inspire new ideas.

The tool is also a step towards making AI music more accessible and engaging. By removing the barrier of entry, Lyria 3 can introduce AI music to a wider audience, potentially leading to a new wave of artistic expression.

## Context & Background

Lyria 3 is a recent addition to the Gemini app, which has become a popular platform for interacting with AI technology. The app has been a huge success, with over 100 million users worldwide.

The release of Lyria 3 is also noteworthy as it coincides with a period of rapid growth in the AI music space. As AI technology becomes more advanced, there is increasing interest in finding new and innovative ways to use AI to create music.

## What to Watch Next

The official launch of Lyria 3 is expected to take place in the coming months. It will be interesting to see how the tool is received by users and what creative projects emerge from it.
