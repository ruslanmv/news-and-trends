## What Happened

Google's AI music maker, Lyria 3, is now available on the Gemini app. This exciting feature allows users to generate 30-second tracks of AI-generated music based on text, images, and videos without leaving the app.

The new music-making tool is the latest addition to the Gemini app's arsenal of creative features. It offers a unique way for users to express themselves and explore new sounds.

## Why It Matters

Lyria 3 is a major milestone in AI-powered music creation. This innovative feature has the potential to revolutionize how users create and interact with music. By making AI-generated music accessible to a wider audience, Google is enabling users to explore a whole new world of possibilities.

## Context & Background

The announcement of Lyria 3 comes at a time when AI is rapidly evolving. The technology is already being used in a variety of applications, from music production to customer service. The Gemini app is a popular platform for creative expression, and the addition of AI music creation is a natural fit.

## What to Watch Next

The official launch date for Lyria 3 is not yet announced. However, Google has confirmed that the feature will be available globally within the next few months. It is expected to be a major hit with Gemini users and music enthusiasts alike.
