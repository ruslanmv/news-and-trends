## What Happened

At the International Onfido Conference (IO 2026), Google unveiled a new line of audio-powered smart glasses called "audio glasses." These devices will allow users to issue verbal commands to them and get things done via the company's vast ecosystem of apps and services, including Google Maps, YouTube, and Gmail.

The announcement comes as Google seeks to take a page out of Meta's book and expand its reach into the smart glasses market. Meta currently owns Instagram and Facebook, two other major players in the social media space. By entering the smart glasses market, Google aims to compete with Apple's Siri and Amazon's Alexa, which have already gained significant market share.

## Why It Matters

The new audio glasses offer several significant benefits, including:

- Hands-free communication: Users can issue verbal commands to the glasses, allowing them to control their devices without having to touch them.
- Streamlined workflow: The glasses can access and control various apps and services, eliminating the need to switch between multiple devices.
- Enhanced entertainment: Users can listen to music, podcasts, and audiobooks hands-free.
- Safety and convenience: The glasses can be worn while driving, providing hands-free access to emergency services or other important information.

## Context & Background

The announcement of Google's new audio glasses comes at a time when the internet of things (IoT) is rapidly expanding, with billions of devices expected to be connected to the internet in the coming years. Smart glasses are a key component of this growing market, and Google's announcement suggests that the company is taking a significant step forward in entering this space.

The announcement also highlights the increasing competition in the smart glasses market. Apple's Siri and Amazon's Alexa are already strong players, and Google is looking to make a significant impact on this market with its new product.

## What to Watch Next

The release of Google's audio glasses is expected to be a major event in the tech world. The device is likely to be unveiled at the company's annual IO conference in June 2026.

Key milestones to watch for include the official launch of the audio glasses, as well as the release of software updates for existing Google devices. It's also worth noting that the success of Google's audio glasses will depend on its ability to compete with Apple and Amazon.
