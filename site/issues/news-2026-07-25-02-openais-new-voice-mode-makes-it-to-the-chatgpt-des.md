## What Happened

OpenAI has launched a new voice mode for its ChatGPT desktop application. This mode allows users to interact with ChatGPT using their voice, making it more accessible and convenient.

The new voice mode supports both ChatGPT Work and Codex, the two main versions of ChatGPT. This means that users can now complete tasks and control agents using their voice, regardless of which version of ChatGPT they are using.

The new voice mode is currently in beta testing and is expected to be released to the public in the coming months.

## Why It Matters

The new voice mode makes ChatGPT much more accessible to a wider range of users. By allowing users to interact with ChatGPT using their voice, it can make it easier for people with cognitive disabilities or those who find it difficult to type to use AI tools.

This new feature also has the potential to increase productivity and creativity. By eliminating the need for users to type, they can focus on generating ideas and completing tasks more quickly.

## Context & Background

The new voice mode is the latest in a series of improvements to ChatGPT. In recent months, OpenAI has been working to make ChatGPT more user-friendly and accessible. These efforts have included the introduction of a text-to-speech option, which allows users to listen to ChatGPT's output in a human voice.

The new voice mode is also a significant milestone for OpenAI. It shows that the company is committed to making ChatGPT accessible to as many people as possible.

## What to Watch Next

The new voice mode is expected to be a major success. It is a significant step forward in making AI more accessible and will hopefully have a positive impact on the way people use AI tools.
