## What Happened

OpenAI has announced that it will start displaying ads on ChatGPT's free and lower-priced Go tiers in India. This move is expected to significantly impact the Indian AI market, as it will allow a wider range of users to access and engage with ChatGPT's powerful AI capabilities.

With over 100 million weekly active ChatGPT users in India, the Go tier is a significant segment of the platform's user base. The integration of advertising will provide users with additional options and incentives to explore the full potential of ChatGPT.

## Why It Matters

This expansion of advertising opportunities will create new revenue streams for OpenAI and its partners. By targeting a broader audience, the company will be able to reach a wider range of potential customers and generate additional revenue. This will also help to further develop the AI ecosystem in India, as it will attract more developers and businesses to invest in the sector.

## Context & Background

The Indian AI market has experienced significant growth in recent years. The country is home to some of the world's largest AI companies, and the country's AI industry is expected to grow at a fast pace in the coming years. This expansion of ChatGPT's advertising capabilities is a key milestone in the growth of the Indian AI market.

## What to Watch Next

The integration of advertising on ChatGPT's free and Go tiers is expected to launch in the coming months. The company has not yet provided any specific timelines or milestones for this launch. However, it is likely that the advertising functionality will be rolled out in phases, with the free and Go tiers being among the first to offer this feature.
