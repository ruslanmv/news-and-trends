## What Happened

Google's Personal Intelligence is getting a major upgrade with the introduction of Gemini AI for Android Auto and cars with Google built-in. This means you can now access your information across all your Google apps, including Gmail, Calendar, Maps, Photos, YouTube, and Search, while driving your car.

**Key Facts:**

* Gemini AI is a conversational AI that helps users with a wide range of tasks, including finding nearby places, checking traffic conditions, and managing your finances.
* This new feature will be available on select Google car models starting in 2026.
* To use the feature, users will simply ask Gemini a question while driving, and the AI will provide them with the answer.

## Why It Matters

This update is significant because it brings the power of Personal Intelligence to a wider audience. Users can now have access to valuable information and assistance while they're driving, improving their overall safety and convenience.

## Context & Background

Google's Personal Intelligence is already a popular feature in Google Assistant, the voice assistant that's integrated into Android devices. This new feature for cars is a natural progression, as many car owners already use Google Assistant to get information and control their vehicles.

## What to Watch Next

The rollout of this feature is expected to be gradual, starting on select Google car models in 2026. Google plans to expand the feature to more models in the future.
