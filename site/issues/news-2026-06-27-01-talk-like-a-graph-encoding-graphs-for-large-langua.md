## What Happened

Google's AI lab unveiled a new service called "Talk Like a Graph" that allows users to interact with large language models (LLMs) through visual graphs. With this technology, users can generate human-quality text, translate languages, and even create simple animations based on their prompts.

The announcement comes as LLMs have gained immense popularity in recent years, with companies like Google, Microsoft, and Facebook investing heavily in research and development. However, the ability to interact with these models in a visual format has been lacking.

"Talk Like a Graph" solves this problem by providing a new way for users to engage with LLMs. The service utilizes a visual interface that allows users to connect and manipulate nodes and edges to create their own conversations with the model.

The service is currently in beta testing, and Google plans to make it available to the public in the coming months.

Talk Like a Graph is a major milestone in the development of AI accessibility. By providing a more intuitive way for users to interact with LLMs, Google is making it easier for people of all backgrounds to learn and use these powerful tools.
