## What Happened

Meta's new AI agent, named "Muse", has taken the tech world by storm. This little robot, designed to spend your money for you, has been causing quite a stir. It can scan the web for deals, make purchases on your behalf, and even remind you of important dates.

## Why It Matters

Muse is a game-changer for consumers. It can help people save time and money, and it can even save them from scams. By automating tedious tasks, Muse allows users to focus on more important things, like spending time with family and friends or pursuing their hobbies.

## Context & Background

Meta's AI unit, known as "Muse", was first announced in 2023. Since then, it has been quietly rolling out to users. The AI is powered by machine learning and is trained on a massive dataset of user behavior and purchases.

## What to Watch Next

As Muse continues to roll out, we can expect to see more features and capabilities added to the AI. Meta has hinted at plans to integrate Muse into other social platforms, such as WhatsApp and Facebook. Additionally, the company has said that they are working on a version of Muse that can help people with disabilities.
