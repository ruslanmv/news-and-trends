## What Happened

Google AI announced the launch of a new tool that could help expand global access to reliable flood forecasts. The tool, called "FloodForecast," uses artificial intelligence to analyze vast amounts of data from various sources, including weather forecasts, satellite imagery, and historical flood records. By combining this data with machine learning algorithms, FloodForecast can predict flood risks with much greater accuracy than traditional forecasting methods.

The tool will be available to governments, humanitarian organizations, and anyone else who needs to make informed decisions about flood mitigation and preparedness.

## Why It Matters

FloodForecast has the potential to save countless lives and property by providing accurate flood forecasts in real-time. This tool could also help to improve the preparedness of communities for flood disasters by identifying areas that are most likely to be affected.

## Context & Background

The tool was developed by a team of scientists at Google AI, in partnership with the World Bank and the United Nations. The project is part of the Global Flood Monitoring System (GFMS), a global network of flood monitoring stations that collect data on floods around the world.

The GFMS is a critical resource for governments and humanitarian organizations working to reduce the impact of floods. The tool will be the first of its kind to be integrated into the GFMS.

## What to Watch Next

The first version of FloodForecast is expected to be released in 2025. The tool will be available to users immediately, and the team plans to continue developing and improving the tool over the next few years.
