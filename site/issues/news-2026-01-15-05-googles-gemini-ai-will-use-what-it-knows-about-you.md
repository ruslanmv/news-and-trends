## What Happened

Google's Gemini AI is getting a significant upgrade that will make it more personalized for users. The company is introducing Personal Intelligence, a feature that will allow users to connect their Gmail, Google Photos, Search, and YouTube history to the chatbot. This means that users can get more relevant answers to their questions and requests.

## Why It Matters

Personal Intelligence is a major upgrade for a number of reasons. It will make it easier for users to find the information they are looking for quickly and easily. It will also help to create a more intimate and responsive user experience.

## Context & Background

Personal Intelligence is Google's latest effort to personalize its AI service. The company has been investing heavily in AI development, and Personal Intelligence is the latest result of that investment. The feature is also part of Google's efforts to make its AI service more accessible and affordable.

## What to Watch Next

Personal Intelligence is expected to be available to all users starting in March 2026. The feature will be rolled out in phases, with the full rollout expected to take six months.

By adding Personal Intelligence to its AI service, Google is taking a major step towards making its AI service more powerful and useful. This feature is sure to be a welcome addition for many users, and it is likely to have a significant impact on the way that people interact with AI.
