## What Happened

OpenAI has released a new subscription tier called ChatGPT Go, making its AI chatbot ChatGPT accessible for a lower price in the US and the rest of the world. The new subscription costs only $10 per month, making it significantly cheaper than the previous pricing of $19 per month.

## Why It Matters

ChatGPT Go is a major milestone in OpenAI's plans to make the AI technology more accessible. This move is expected to attract a wider range of users, including businesses, schools, and individuals who may not have been able to afford ChatGPT in the past.

## Context & Background

OpenAI's ChatGPT has become a major cultural phenomenon since its launch in 2026. The AI language model has been used for various tasks, including language translation, question answering, and content creation.

## What to Watch Next

The launch of ChatGPT Go is expected to have a significant impact on the AI industry. This lower-priced subscription tier is likely to attract a wider range of users, which could lead to increased demand for ChatGPT services and products. It could also help to further democratize AI technology and make it more accessible for people around the world.
