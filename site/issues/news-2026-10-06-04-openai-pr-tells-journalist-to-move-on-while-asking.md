## What Happened

OpenAI publicist, Sarah Adler, attempted to redirect the topic of CEO Sam Altman's upcoming interview with Vanity Fair's Mark Guiducci after learning about the recent death of a ChatGPT user. When Guiducci pressed Altman on the matter, Adler cut him off, stating she needed to "move on" to another topic.

 Altman's interview had been centered around his views on artificial intelligence and the potential impact on society. However, the discussion took a tragic turn when Guiducci pressed him to comment on the user's death, prompting Adler to intervene.
