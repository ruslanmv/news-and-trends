## What Happened

Anthropic's Mythos AI preview is a powerful tool that could significantly boost the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA)'s ability to identify and mitigate cyber vulnerabilities. However, the agency is reportedly left out of access to the tool, leaving a gap in the nation's cybersecurity defense.

The Mythos Preview is a platform that allows cybersecurity professionals to analyze and test their systems against real-world cyberattacks. This access would have allowed CISA to evaluate the effectiveness of Anthropic's tool and determine its potential to improve its security posture.

## Why It Matters

The absence of access to Mythos Preview will limit CISA's ability to assess the effectiveness of Anthropic's tool and prevent potential cyberattacks. This could leave the nation vulnerable to a variety of attacks, including ransomware, phishing, and social engineering.

## Context & Background

Anthropic's Mythos AI is a machine learning-based vulnerability assessment tool. The tool analyzes and tests systems against real-world cyberattacks, identifying potential vulnerabilities that attackers could exploit. Anthropic's tool has been shown to be effective in identifying vulnerabilities that would be missed by traditional security tools.

CISA's decision to leave Mythos Preview out of access is a concerning move that could put the nation at risk. The tool is a valuable asset for preventing cyberattacks, and its absence will create a security gap that could be exploited by attackers.

## What to Watch Next

CISA will continue to explore alternative options for cybersecurity tools. The agency is also expected to release updates to Mythos Preview on a regular basis. It will be important to monitor these developments to ensure that the tool is being utilized effectively.
