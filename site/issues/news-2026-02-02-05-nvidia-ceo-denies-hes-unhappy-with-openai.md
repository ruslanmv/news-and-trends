## What Happened

Nvidia's CEO, Jensen Huang, has denied reports that he is unhappy with OpenAI. The company recently announced an investment of up to $100 billion in OpenAI, but recent rumors suggest that the deal might not proceed.

Huang stated that he is committed to "making the right decisions" regarding OpenAI and that the company still plans to invest in the AI firm.

## Why It Matters

The OpenAI deal is a major milestone in the AI industry, and this news could have a significant impact on the future of the company. The investment is expected to accelerate OpenAI's growth and development, and it could also lead to the creation of new jobs.

## Context & Background

The OpenAI deal is a major innovation in the field of artificial intelligence. OpenAI is a large language model that has been trained on a massive dataset of text and code. This model has the potential to revolutionize a wide range of industries, including healthcare, finance, and manufacturing.
