The announcement came as no surprise, as ChatGPT has been experimenting with various forms of AI-generated content, including erotic art and music, in recent months.

The development of ChatGPT's erotic mode also raised concerns about the potential for misuse, with some individuals expressing interest in using it for harmful purposes. OpenAI took steps to address these concerns, but the model's development and deployment were ultimately cut short.

ChatGPT's erotic mode is not the first side project to face scrutiny. The future of ChatGPT and its erotic mode remains uncertain. However, the company has stated that it will continue to explore the ethical and societal implications of AI-generated content.
