## What Happened

The Nvidia, Microsoft, SpaceX, IBM, and other tech companies have launched an Open Secure AI Alliance to address the growing concerns over the safety of AI systems. This new alliance aims to provide open-source AI security tools to help developers and users build and deploy secure and trustworthy AI solutions.

The Open Secure AI Alliance is a direct response to the growing threat of frontier models – advanced AI systems that can bypass existing security solutions. These models pose a significant challenge to security researchers and developers, as they can be used to automate complex tasks and make malicious attacks more efficient.

## Why It Matters

The Open Secure AI Alliance is a significant step towards making AI more secure. By providing open-source tools, the alliance aims to encourage collaboration and innovation in the AI security community. This will help developers identify and address vulnerabilities in AI systems more effectively, leading to a safer future for all.

## Context & Background

The Open Secure AI Alliance is a new initiative that was announced during a conference held in June 2026. The initiative was met with positive feedback from the AI community, with many experts expressing their hope that the alliance would help to make AI more secure.

The AI security landscape is constantly evolving, with new threats and vulnerabilities emerging all the time. The Open Secure AI Alliance is a timely effort to address this challenge and create a more secure future for AI.

## What to Watch Next

The Open Secure AI Alliance is expected to make significant progress in the coming months. The first phase of the initiative will focus on developing open-source tools for AI security. These tools will be available to the public through a dedicated website, and will be used to build and deploy secure AI systems.

The second phase of the initiative will focus on promoting the use of these tools and educating developers and users about the importance of AI security. The alliance will also work with industry partners to develop new standards and best practices for AI security.
