## What Happened

IBM has partnered with OpenAI to expand its enterprise AI push. The companies will collaborate on training and certifying tens of thousands of consultants on OpenAI's AI technologies, with the goal of accelerating the adoption of AI in various industries.

IBM's initiative signifies a significant investment in AI education and upskilling, reflecting the increasing demand for skilled AI professionals in the market.

## Why It Matters

This partnership is crucial for several reasons. First, it enables IBM to leverage OpenAI's cutting-edge AI solutions, expanding its product and service offerings to encompass AI capabilities. Second, it expands OpenAI's reach into the enterprise space, providing access to a vast pool of skilled consultants that can benefit various industries. Third, this collaboration fosters innovation and accelerates the development of new AI solutions, benefiting both IBM and OpenAI.

## Context & Background

IBM has been a leading technology company for decades and has consistently pushed the boundaries of innovation. OpenAI is a rapidly growing AI company that specializes in natural language processing and computer vision. The partnership underscores IBM's commitment to embracing AI and integrating it into its core business operations.

## What to Watch Next

The exact timeline for this partnership is not provided in the news, but it is expected to be finalized within the next year. The collaboration is expected to have a significant impact on the AI landscape, leading to increased adoption and innovation across industries.
