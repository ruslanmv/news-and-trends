## What Happened

IBM and University of Chicago researchers have achieved a landmark milestone in the field of quantum computing by solving a classically intractable problem in just 15 minutes - a feat that would have been impossible with classical methods. The system, which utilized 70 error-corrected logical qubits, achieved this remarkable result by harnessing the quantum properties of these quantum bits to significantly reduce the computational complexity of the task.

## Why It Matters

This breakthrough has significant implications for various industries and markets. It opens up new possibilities for drug discovery, materials science, and financial modeling, among other fields. By significantly accelerating the solution of complex problems, this technology could lead to breakthroughs in artificial intelligence, drug discovery, and technological advancements.

## Context & Background

The rapid advancements in quantum computing technology have sparked a global debate about its potential impact on society. While some experts believe it could revolutionize various industries, others raise concerns about the ethical and economic implications of this technology.

## What to Watch Next

The research team plans to continue exploring the potential applications of their breakthrough and hopes that their findings can lead to the development of new technologies and products in the near future.
