## What Happened

Google's AI team unveiled a new technique called "graph embedding" that can encode and understand large language models (LLMs) in a new way. This approach offers several advantages over previous methods, including the ability to handle complex and contextual language more effectively.

The new method is based on a technique called "structural deep learning," which focuses on analyzing the relationships between different concepts and entities in a graph. This approach allows Google to capture the semantic meaning of language more accurately, resulting in better performance on tasks such as text generation, translation, and question answering.
