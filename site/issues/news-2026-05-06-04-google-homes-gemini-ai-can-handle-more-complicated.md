## What Happened

Google has updated its smart home assistant, Google Home, to version 3.1. This upgrade introduces several new features and capabilities, including the ability to handle more complex and multi-step requests.

The new version of Home is more capable of interpreting and responding to complex and multi-step commands, allowing users to combine various tasks into a single, seamless command. This feature is particularly useful for users with smart home devices, as it allows them to control multiple devices with a single voice command.

The update also introduces several new features, including the ability to create and share routines, as well as the ability to control smart home devices from anywhere using the Google Home app.

## Why It Matters

The new version of Google Home is significant for a number of reasons:

- It makes it easier for users to control their smart home devices.
- It allows users to control multiple devices with a single voice command.
- It introduces new features that were previously not available on Google Home.

This upgrade is a major step forward for Google Home and is likely to be very well received by users. It will make it easier for users to control their smart home devices and create more efficient and convenient workflows.

## Context & Background

Google Home is a smart home assistant that was released by Google in 2016. The device is compatible with a wide range of smart home devices, including lights, thermostats, and speakers. Home is controlled through a mobile app or a physical remote.

The launch of Google Home was a major milestone in the smart home industry. The device was one of the first smart home devices to offer a seamless and integrated experience. Home has since been a very popular device, and it is now used by millions of people worldwide.
