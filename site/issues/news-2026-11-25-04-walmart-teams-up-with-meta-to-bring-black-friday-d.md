## What Happened

Walmart has partnered with Meta to allow customers to shop Black Friday deals through Muse, Meta's AI agent. This integration enables users to browse and purchase products through Muse's AI capabilities, providing a more personalized and convenient shopping experience.

## Why It Matters

This collaboration marks a significant step in retail innovation, leveraging AI technology to enhance the shopping process. By integrating Black Friday deals with Muse, Walmart aims to provide customers with an even more engaging and efficient shopping experience. This integration also opens new possibilities for personalized recommendations and targeted offers, potentially leading to increased sales and customer satisfaction.

## Context & Background

The partnership between Walmart and Meta is a testament to the growing influence of AI in the retail industry. As AI technology advances, retailers are exploring innovative ways to leverage AI to personalize the customer experience and optimize their operations. This collaboration reflects this commitment, with Walmart and Meta joining forces to bring Black Friday deals to a wider audience through Muse's AI capabilities.

## What to Watch Next

The integration of Black Friday deals through Muse is expected to launch in the coming weeks, allowing customers to shop on Black Friday with the assistance of AI technology. This collaboration is likely to shape the future of retail, demonstrating the potential for AI to transform the shopping experience for both retailers and customers.
