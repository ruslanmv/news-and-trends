## What Happened

Meta has signed a massive deal with Nvidia to expand its data centers with millions of its Grace and Vera CPUs and Blackwell and Rubin GPUs. This represents the first large-scale Nvidia Grace-only deployment, which will bring significant performance-per-watt improvements to Meta's AI products.

The collaboration is expected to lead to increased efficiency and performance of Meta's AI infrastructure, which is crucial for supporting the rapid growth of its AI products.

## Why It Matters

This deal marks a significant milestone in the evolution of artificial intelligence (AI). The increased capacity of Meta's data centers will allow them to handle more complex AI models and datasets, enabling them to develop more advanced and efficient AI products. The collaboration will also bring down the cost of AI development, making it more accessible for businesses and consumers.

## Context & Background

Meta's AI business has grown rapidly in recent years, and the company has been one of the leading adopters of Nvidia's hardware. This deal is a testament to the growing partnership between Meta and Nvidia, and it is expected to have a significant impact on the future of AI development.

## What to Watch Next

The collaboration between Meta and Nvidia is expected to lead to the development of new AI products and services. Meta is already using Nvidia's hardware for its AI products, and this deal will allow them to scale their AI operations to even greater heights. The partnership is also expected to bring new technologies and innovations to the AI industry, which will benefit all stakeholders.
