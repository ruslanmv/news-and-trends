## What Happened

The article introduces the use of artificial intelligence to expand global access to reliable flood forecasts. This technology has the potential to revolutionize flood management by providing flood forecasters with access to a wider range of data sources and by automating the flood forecasting process. 

The announcement was made by Google AI, a global leader in artificial intelligence research, and the article highlights the collaboration between Google and the World Weather Watch organization.

## Why It Matters

The development of AI flood forecasting has significant implications for several key areas:

* **Enhanced accuracy:** AI can analyze and interpret a vast amount of data sources, including weather patterns, satellite images, and ground observations, to produce more accurate flood forecasts than traditional methods.
* **Increased accessibility:** By providing flood forecasts to communities in real-time, AI can help to improve disaster preparedness and response.
* **Improved decision-making:** AI-powered flood forecasts can provide valuable insights to policymakers and emergency management personnel, enabling them to make informed decisions about flood mitigation and evacuation.

## Context & Background

The article highlights the increasing frequency and severity of floods around the world. Traditional flood forecasting methods are often limited by the availability of data and the difficulty of accessing remote areas. AI technology offers a potential solution to this problem.

## What to Watch Next

The article expects the development and deployment of a new AI-powered flood forecasting system within the next 5 years. This system is expected to be more accurate and reliable than traditional methods, and it is hoped to be widely available to communities around the world.
