## What Happened

IBM and University of Chicago researchers have achieved a groundbreaking milestone in quantum computing by solving a long-standing problem in 15 minutes. The breakthrough system utilized 70 error-corrected logical qubits and achieved the result despite the challenges posed by the physical limitations of the quantum hardware.

This remarkable achievement signifies the potential of quantum computing to tackle complex problems that were previously deemed intractable. It has significant implications for various industries, including healthcare, finance, and materials science.

## Why It Matters

The significance of this discovery lies in its vast potential to revolutionize numerous fields. It solves a long-standing problem in quantum computing, paving the way for further advancements in fields like drug discovery, materials science, and artificial intelligence.

## Context & Background

Quantum computing is a rapidly evolving field with immense potential to transform our world. Quantum computers possess the ability to process vast amounts of data exponentially faster than classical computers, potentially solving problems intractable for classical computers.

This breakthrough also marks a significant milestone in the quest to build a quantum computer that can rival the human brain. The technology has the potential to lead to breakthroughs in various fields, including medicine, finance, and materials science.
