## What Happened

Google TV has recently introduced three new Gemini-powered features that are sure to keep fans updated on their favorite sports teams. These features include visual responses, deep dives, and sports briefs.

Visual responses allow users to ask Google TV questions about a specific game or player, and the device will provide a video answer in real-time. Deep dives offer a comprehensive look at a team or player, including their stats, news, and upcoming schedule. Sports briefs provide a quick overview of the current game, including the score, standings, and upcoming events.
