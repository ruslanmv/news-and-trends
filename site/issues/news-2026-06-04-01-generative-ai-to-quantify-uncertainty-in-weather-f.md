The Google AI Blog announces the development of a new tool called Generative AI, which can quantify uncertainty in weather forecasting. The tool uses machine learning to analyze historical weather data and predict future weather patterns with greater accuracy than traditional forecasting methods.

By quantifying uncertainty, Generative AI can help to improve decision-making and preparedness for extreme weather events.
