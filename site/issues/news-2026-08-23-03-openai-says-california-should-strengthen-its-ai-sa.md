## What Happened

OpenAI has called on California to strengthen the state's Artificial Intelligence Safety Bill (SB 53). The bill, which was previously opposed by OpenAI, aims to address the growing risks associated with AI, particularly in the context of large language models like ChatGPT and its potential misuse.

SB 53 requires companies working with AI to implement robust safety measures, including human oversight, transparency, and risk assessment. The bill also establishes a new oversight body responsible for monitoring and enforcing these safety measures.

## Why It Matters

The bill is crucial for several reasons. It will help to ensure the safe development, deployment, and use of AI in California. By imposing strict safety measures, the bill will help to prevent the harmful consequences of AI, such as bias, misinformation, and misuse.

The bill is also a significant step towards establishing a regulatory framework for AI in the United States. California's strong stance on this issue is likely to influence the federal government's approach to AI regulation.

## Context & Background

California has been a leader in establishing policies related to AI safety. In 2021, the state passed AB 1725, which requires companies operating AI systems over $1 billion to comply with the bill's provisions.

The bill's passage has sparked debate among technologists and policymakers. Some argue that SB 53 is too burdensome and that it could stifle innovation. Others contend that it is a necessary step to protect the public from the risks associated with AI.

## What to Watch Next

The California legislature is expected to vote on SB 53 this fall. If the bill passes, it will become law and will be implemented by the state's Department of Consumer Affairs.

The bill's implementation will be closely watched by industry leaders, policymakers, and the public. The outcome of this vote will have significant implications for the future of AI development and use in California and beyond.
