## Using AI to Expand Global Access to Reliable Flood Forecasts

**What Happened:**

Google AI unveiled a new platform called "Flood Action" that leverages artificial intelligence (AI) to predict and track floods with unprecedented accuracy and speed. This global initiative aims to provide real-time, localized flood forecasts, benefiting communities worldwide.

**Why It Matters:**

Flood forecasts have historically been limited in accuracy and coverage, particularly in underserved regions. This new platform promises significant improvements by utilizing advanced AI algorithms to analyze vast amounts of data, including satellite imagery, weather forecasts, and ground observations. By collaborating with over 150 international partners, the project aims to create the most comprehensive and accurate flood forecast system to date.

**Context & Background:**

The increasing frequency and severity of extreme weather events, such as floods, poses a major threat to human lives, infrastructure, and economies worldwide. The project addresses this challenge by empowering local communities and decision-makers with real-time flood risk information. 

**What to Watch Next:**

The Flood Action platform is expected to launch in early 2024 and will be initially available in select regions. The project aims to expand globally, with the ultimate goal of providing forecasts for all major river systems and major lakes.
