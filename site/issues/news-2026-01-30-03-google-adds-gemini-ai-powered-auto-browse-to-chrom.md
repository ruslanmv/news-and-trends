## What Happened

Google is launching a new "auto browse" feature inside Chrome that can perform multi-step tasks on your behalf. The Gemini AI-powered capability is coming to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the US, and can do things like research hotel and flight costs, schedule appointments, fill out online forms, manage subscriptions, and more. This feature is designed to save users time and effort by automating tedious tasks and providing them with a single point of access to manage their personal and professional affairs.

## Why It Matters

The auto browse feature is a significant milestone in Google's AI development. This technology is intended to offer a more comprehensive and convenient user experience by combining the capabilities of various existing tools and services into a single, unified platform. By streamlining the process of managing personal and professional tasks, Google hopes to attract new users to the Chrome ecosystem and encourage existing users to upgrade to a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription.

## Context & Background

The auto browse feature is the latest addition to Google's already impressive suite of AI-powered features for Chrome. Google has been investing heavily in AI research and development, and the company has made significant progress in this field in recent years. The auto browse feature is a testament to Google's commitment to providing users with the best possible experience.

## What to Watch Next

The official release date for the auto browse feature has not been announced by Google. However, it is expected to launch in the coming months. Users who are interested in learning more about the feature can sign up for the Google AI Pro or Ultra program, which offers access to the latest AI features.
