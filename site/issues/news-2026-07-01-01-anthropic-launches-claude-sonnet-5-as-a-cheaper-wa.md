## What Happened

Anthropic has officially unveiled Claude Sonnet 5, a cost-effective variant of their already impressive GPT-5 rival, Claude. The new model boasts several significant improvements, including:

* **Enhanced agency capabilities:** Claude Sonnet 5 offers an even stronger foundation for AI agents, making them more versatile and efficient.
* **Lower pricing:** The new model is priced affordably, making it a more accessible option for individuals and businesses with limited budgets.
* **Improved safety:** Claude Sonnet 5 incorporates advanced safety features to enhance its robustness and prevent misuse.

These enhancements solidify Claude Sonnet 5’s position as a formidable competitor in the AI landscape, offering a compelling balance between affordability and performance.
