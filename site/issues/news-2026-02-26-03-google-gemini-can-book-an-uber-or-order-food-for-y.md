## What Happened

Google's Gemini AI is taking a major step towards becoming a more comprehensive and helpful assistant. Starting with the Pixel 10 and Galaxy S26 series, Gemini will be able to book an Uber or order food for you on its own with a simple voice command.

This innovative feature is called task automation, and it essentially allows users to control their devices with just a few words. The technology utilizes natural language processing (NLP) to understand and execute requests, making it much easier to interact with your phone or tablet.

This advancement has the potential to significantly impact the way we live and work. By automating tasks, we can save time and effort, allowing us to focus on more important and creative pursuits.

The introduction of task automation is a major milestone in the development of AI. By making it easier for users to interact with their devices, Google is taking a step towards creating a truly integrated and seamless experience. This feature is particularly useful for busy individuals and families who often have multiple devices to manage.

The implementation of task automation also has broader implications for the industry. The announcement of task automation comes at a time when AI is rapidly evolving.
