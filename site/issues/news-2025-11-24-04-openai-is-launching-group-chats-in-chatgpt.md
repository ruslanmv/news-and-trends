## What Happened

OpenAI has officially rolled out group chats within its ChatGPT platform, allowing users to invite up to 20 other people to engage in a conversation with the AI. This feature is now available globally to all logged-in users, marking a significant expansion for the AI chatbot.

## Why It Matters

This feature is a game-changer for several reasons:

* **Enhanced collaboration:** Group chats facilitate collaborative brainstorming, idea sharing, and decision-making.
* **Increased accessibility:** The ability to engage in conversations with ChatGPT expands its reach and accessibility, especially for groups and organizations.
* **Improved engagement:** Group chats can foster deeper and more meaningful conversations, leading to richer and more insightful outcomes.

## Context & Background

This feature aligns with OpenAI's ongoing efforts to enhance ChatGPT's capabilities and foster wider adoption. Group chats are increasingly becoming a popular communication medium, particularly among tech enthusiasts and businesses seeking AI solutions.

## What to Watch Next

The launch of group chats is a significant milestone in ChatGPT's evolution. The company plans to introduce additional features and integrations in the future, further expanding the possibilities for collaborative AI experiences.

This feature holds immense potential to revolutionize how people interact with AI, fostering more engaging and productive conversations across various domains, including business, education, and entertainment.
