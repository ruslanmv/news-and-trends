## Using AI to Expand Global Access to Reliable Flood Forecasts

**What Happened**: Google has developed a machine learning model that can expand access to real-time flood forecasts by analyzing global weather data and weather patterns. This AI tool, called "FloodPredict," can provide flood alerts to communities in real-time, regardless of their location or internet access.

**Why It Matters**: The model can significantly improve disaster preparedness and response by allowing authorities to allocate resources effectively and prevent devastating floods. By providing timely warnings and accurate flood predictions, FloodPredict can save lives and property.

**Context & Background**: The model was developed by a team of engineers and scientists at Google and the Weather Channel. It is the latest in a series of AI tools developed by Google to address a wide range of global challenges.

**What to Watch Next**: The model is currently in beta testing and is expected to be available to the public in the coming months. This will allow for widespread testing and feedback, with the ultimate goal of expanding its reach to all flood-prone regions worldwide.
