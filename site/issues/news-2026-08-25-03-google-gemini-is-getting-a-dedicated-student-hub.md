## What Happened

Google's Gemini platform is getting a major upgrade with the launch of a dedicated student hub. This new hub is a one-stop solution for students, offering a range of tools and resources to help them learn more effectively.

**Key facts**:

- The student hub is accessible through the Google Meet app.
- It features a study notebook with support for graphs and images.
- It also includes practice quizzes and test date tracking.
- The hub is designed to help students stay organized and motivated.

## Why It Matters

The student hub is a significant development for Google's education platform. It will help to:

- Make learning more convenient and accessible for students.
- Help students to stay organized and focused.
- Provide students with a valuable resource to learn from anywhere.

## Context & Background

Google's Gemini platform is a suite of tools designed to help educators and students connect and collaborate. The platform includes Google Meet, Classroom, and now, the student hub.

Gemini is used by over 10 million people in over 100 countries. The platform is constantly evolving, with new features being added all the time.

## What to Watch Next

As the student hub is a new feature, there is not much information available about what to expect next. However, it is likely that Google will continue to add new features to the platform. This could include features such as a virtual library, a social network, and more.
