## What Happened

Meta AI director Rohit Patel has left the company to take on the role of Microsoft Office and Google Workspace. Patel, who has been with Meta for eight years, will oversee the development and implementation of the company's AI systems for Office and Workspace.

## Why It Matters

Meta's AI systems are used by millions of employees across its platforms, including Meta Workspace and Office. Patel's departure is significant as he has played a key role in shaping and expanding Meta's AI capabilities. His new role will undoubtedly have a major impact on the company's overall AI strategy.

## Context & Background

Meta's AI systems have been a major focus for the company, and Patel played a central role in expanding and refining them. He was instrumental in the development of Meta's AI platform and the deployment of AI tools across the company's products and services.

## What to Watch Next

Patel's new role is expected to have a major impact on the development and implementation of Meta's AI systems for Office and Workspace. His expertise and leadership will be invaluable to the continued success of these platforms.
