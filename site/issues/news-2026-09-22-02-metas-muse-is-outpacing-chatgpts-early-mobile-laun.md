## What Happened

Meta's latest AI venture, Muse, has surpassed the initial mobile launch of ChatGPT in the U.S. and Canada, with more than twice the daily active users. This surge in popularity follows Meta's recent investments in AI research and development.

According to Appfigures, Muse has already surpassed the 10 million downloads mark, outperforming ChatGPT's initial 4 million downloads within the same period.

## Why It Matters

Muse's rapid growth signifies a major milestone in AI development for Meta. The company is actively expanding its AI workforce and focusing heavily on conversational AI. By successfully capturing the market share from ChatGPT, Meta can further accelerate its AI ambitions and explore new avenues like voice and video interactions.

The increased user base also highlights the growing demand for AI solutions across various industries and applications.

## Context & Background

Meta's recent investments in AI have been driven by the increasing demand for AI solutions across multiple industries. AI has the potential to revolutionize various aspects of society, including healthcare, education, and entertainment.

The recent surge in AI activity has sparked concerns about the ethical and societal implications of advanced AI. As AI technology advances, it's crucial to address these concerns and ensure its responsible development and use.

## What to Watch Next

With Muse's rapid growth, we can expect further advancements in AI-powered products and services from Meta. The company is expected to continue investing in AI research and development, collaborating with researchers and developers from various institutions. Additionally, Meta is likely to leverage its vast resources to expand its AI reach into emerging markets.
