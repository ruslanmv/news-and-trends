## What Happened

OpenAI's first hardware release will be a smart speaker with a camera that will probably cost between $200 and $300, according to The Information. The device will be able to recognize things like "items on a nearby table or conversations people are having in the vicinity," The Information says, and it will have a Face detection feature that can recognize people in the room. The speaker will also have a voice assistant, allowing users to control the device with their voice.

## Why It Matters

The main significance of this product is its ability to offer a hands-free way to interact with technology. By using voice commands, users can control the smart speaker without needing to touch it or type on a screen. This could be a game-changer for people with physical disabilities, seniors, or those who have trouble reading or writing.

## Context & Background

The smart speaker is a relatively new type of technology, and OpenAI is one of the leading companies developing these devices. The company has already released several successful smart speakers, but this is the first that includes a camera. This camera is designed to be used for a variety of purposes, such as security, home automation, and entertainment.

## What to Watch Next

The first OpenAI smart speaker with a camera is expected to launch in 2027. The speaker will be priced around $200 and will have a range of features, including:

- Voice assistant with natural language processing (NLP)
- Face detection
- Object recognition
- Environmental monitoring
- Home automation capabilities
