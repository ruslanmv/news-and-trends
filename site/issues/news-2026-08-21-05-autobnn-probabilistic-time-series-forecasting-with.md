## What Happened

AutoBNN is a new research project focused on probabilistic time series forecasting using compositional Bayesian neural networks. This approach combines the power of probabilistic modeling with the efficiency of neural networks, potentially leading to more accurate predictions compared to traditional methods.

The project, spearheaded by researchers at Google AI, has achieved promising results. They showcased the efficacy of AutoBNN on various datasets, demonstrating its ability to generate high-quality forecasts for various time series problems.

The key to the innovation lies in the combination of two core techniques: probabilistic modeling and neural networks. This approach allows the model to incorporate the inherent uncertainty and non-linearity in the data, resulting in more robust and accurate predictions.
