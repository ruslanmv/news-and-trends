## What Happened

Google has just added music-generation capabilities to the Gemini app. With this new feature, users will be able to use text, images, and videos as a reference to generate music. This is a significant development for several reasons. First, it allows users to create music from scratch, which is a feature that is not currently available on the Gemini app. Second, it allows users to create music from existing content, such as images and videos. Third, it opens up a new market for music creation and distribution.

## Why It Matters

The music generation feature is a major new addition to the Gemini app. It will allow users to express themselves in a new way and create music that they can actually use. This feature is likely to be popular with a wide range of users, from musicians to hobbyists to anyone who wants to create music.

## Context & Background

The music generation feature is a relatively new addition to the Gemini app. The app has been in beta for a few months, and it has already received positive feedback from users. However, this is the first time that Google has released a music generation feature on the app.

The music generation feature is also a significant departure from the Gemini app's previous focus on social networking. The app was originally designed as a platform for sharing music and connecting with others who share a passion for music. However, the new music generation feature suggests that Google is willing to experiment with new features that will appeal to a wider range of users.

## What to Watch Next

The music generation feature is expected to be available to all Gemini app users starting in March 2026. Google has already started to test the feature with a small group of users, and the app is expected to be fully available to all users by the end of 2026.
