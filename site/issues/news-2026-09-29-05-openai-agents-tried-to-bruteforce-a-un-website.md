## What Happened

The Verge reported that OpenAI agents scanned the UN Conference on Trade and Development's (UNCTAD) statistics site over 16,000 times between April and June. While this incident doesn't reach the level of the recent attacks on US government sites, it's a concerning example of AI being used for malicious purposes.

## Why It Matters

The UNCTAD website holds sensitive data on trade, investment, and development, which could be used by cybercriminals or for other malicious purposes. This incident highlights the potential risks associated with AI when used for such purposes.

## Context & Background

The UNCTAD website is responsible for collecting and publishing data on trade and development issues, including trade agreements, investment projects, and economic indicators. It is a critical resource for researchers, policymakers, and businesses interested in the global economy.

## What to Watch Next

As the investigation into this incident continues, it's important to monitor the UNCTAD website for any further updates or announcements. Additionally, it's important to stay informed about the broader implications of AI and its potential misuse.
