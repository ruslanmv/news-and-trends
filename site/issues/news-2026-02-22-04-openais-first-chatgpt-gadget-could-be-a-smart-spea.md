## What Happened

OpenAI has announced the release of its first hardware product, a smart speaker with a camera. The device, which will cost between $200 and $300, is expected to be released in the second half of 2026.

The speaker will be able to recognize things like "items on a nearby table or conversations people are having in the vicinity," The Information says, and it will have a facial recognition feature. It's also equipped with a microphone for listening and video recording.

## Why It Matters

The launch of this smart speaker is significant because it marks the first time OpenAI has entered the hardware market. The company is already known for its AI software, but this device shows that it is also looking to expand into the hardware space.

The smart speaker also has the potential to be a major competitor to other smart speakers on the market. With its advanced features and competitive price, it could become a popular choice for consumers looking for a smart speaker that can do more than just listen to music.

## Context & Background

The announcement of the smart speaker was made at the annual OpenAI conference, which took place in March 2026. The conference is the world's largest gathering of AI professionals, and it is a major platform for the company to announce new products and developments.

The smart speaker is also a major milestone in OpenAI's history. The company has been developing AI software for over a decade, and this device is the first product that it has released that is not purely software. This move indicates that OpenAI is taking its hardware business seriously, and it is willing to invest heavily in developing new products.

## What to Watch Next

The future of the smart speaker market is bright. With the increasing popularity of AI, we can expect to see a number of new products and developments in this space in the coming years. Some of the most likely developments include:

- More smart speakers with advanced features, such as voice control and facial recognition.
- A wider range of smart speakers that are designed for specific purposes, such as smart speakers for children or smart speakers that are waterproof.
- The development of new AI technologies that can be used to power smart speakers, such as natural language processing and computer vision.
