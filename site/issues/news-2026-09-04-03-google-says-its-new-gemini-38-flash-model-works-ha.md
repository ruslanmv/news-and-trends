## What Happened

The Verge's latest article dives into Google's newest innovation, the Gemini 3.8 Flash model, which promises to be more capable than its predecessor. The new model boasts improved reasoning capabilities thanks to an iterative call approach, enabling it to handle more complex tasks.

The cost of the Gemini 3.8 Flash remains the same as its predecessor, $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens.

This advancement marks a significant milestone for Google's AI division, showcasing their commitment to pushing the boundaries of AI technology.
