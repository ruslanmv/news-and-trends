## What Happened

Google has released Gemini 4 Argon, a new AI model designed to be "so capable" that it can only be accessed by "trusted cyber defenders." The model can handle complex workflows across a range of software engineering, enterprise knowledge work, legal, and financial sectors while protecting against cybersecurity threats.

However, due to its power and capabilities, Google has restricted access to Gemini 4 Argon to ensure its safety and prevent misuse. The company plans to release the model in phases, with the first phase opening to only "trusted customers" and developers.
