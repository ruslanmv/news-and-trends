## What Happened

The Verge reported that Google won't be replacing Assistant with Gemini on Android devices by the end of 2025 as initially planned. Instead, the company will continue its work to upgrade Assistant users to Gemini on mobile devices in 2026.

## Why It Matters

The announcement is significant as it delays the long-awaited switch to Gemini for Assistant users on Android devices. This shift was initially planned to happen by the end of 2025. This delay is likely due to technical challenges and ensuring a smooth transition for users.

## Context & Background

Google's decision to delay the switch to Gemini marks a significant moment in the evolution of Android operating systems. The company has consistently pushed the update to Android devices, but technical hurdles and the importance of maintaining existing functionalities have often hindered the rollout.

## What to Watch Next

The Verge reports that Google plans to release more information about the Gemini upgrade in the coming months. This could include the specific features and improvements to be expected, as well as any potential timeline updates. Additionally, the company may provide more context about the reasons for the delay.
