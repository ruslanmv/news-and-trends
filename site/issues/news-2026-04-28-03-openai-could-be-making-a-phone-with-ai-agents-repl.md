## What Happened

The news reveals that OpenAI is planning to release a phone featuring AI agents replacing apps. This announcement comes from industry analyst Ming-Chi Kuo, who stated that the company is collaborating with MediaTek, Qualcomm, and Luxshare on a phone project.

The phone is expected to be released in the second half of 2027 and will be the first smartphone to integrate AI agents directly into the device. These agents are designed to take over various tasks, including setting reminders, playing music, and controlling smart home devices.

This collaboration suggests a significant investment in the future of mobile technology. By combining OpenAI's advanced AI technology with these established companies, the phone is poised to offer a unique and transformative user experience.
