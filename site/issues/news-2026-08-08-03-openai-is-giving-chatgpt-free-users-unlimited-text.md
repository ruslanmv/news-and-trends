## What Happened

OpenAI has announced a major change for its free and Go tiers, allowing users to have unlimited text chats with the chatbot. This move marks a significant shift in how users interact with ChatGPT, expanding its reach and potential.

## Why It Matters

With the introduction of unlimited text chats, OpenAI aims to remove potential limitations imposed by rate limits, opening up new possibilities for users. This move is a game-changer for content creators, allowing them to generate vast amounts of text without facing throttling issues.

## Context & Background

This news comes at a time when ChatGPT is rapidly gaining popularity and influence within various industries. As a language model with remarkable capabilities, ChatGPT can assist with a wide range of tasks, from writing and translation to code generation and information retrieval.

## What to Watch Next

The implementation of unlimited text chats on the free and Go tiers is expected to be gradual, with OpenAI gradually expanding its reach to encompass more users. The company has also promised to provide more granular control options for users to manage their chat experience.
