## What Happened

IBM and University of Chicago researchers have achieved a breakthrough in quantum computing by solving a problem that classical methods could not practically tackle in 15 minutes. The system, which used 70 error-corrected logical qubits, achieved this remarkable feat by statistically verifying the result's reliability.

This groundbreaking achievement has significant implications for various industries and markets, including:

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** This breakthrough can lead to more efficient and robust AI algorithms, paving the way for advancements in areas such as drug discovery and financial modeling.


- **Cryptography:** Quantum computing has the potential to revolutionize cybersecurity by breaking current encryption methods. However, it also creates opportunities for developing new, more robust encryption solutions.


- **Quantum Chemistry:** This discovery could lead to new breakthroughs in quantum chemistry, providing a deeper understanding of molecular structures and reactions.
