## What Happened

A new Google commercial imagines the founding fathers using the latest AI collaboration tools and tools to draft the Declaration of Independence. The commercial portrays a 1770s-style brainstorming session between Thomas Jefferson, Benjamin Franklin, and others, working together seamlessly on the document.

## Why It Matters

The commercial highlights the transformative potential of AI in bridging historical divides and fostering collaboration. It demonstrates how AI tools can be used to facilitate communication, idea sharing, and consensus-building, even among individuals with diverse backgrounds and perspectives.

## Context & Background

The commercial takes place against the backdrop of growing concerns about misinformation and the increasing use of AI in media and decision-making. It underscores the need for responsible development and use of AI technologies and emphasizes the importance of harnessing their potential for positive social impact.

## What to Watch Next

The commercial's release sparks discussions about the ethical implications of AI in historical moments, and raises questions about how we can ensure that AI is used responsibly and inclusively in the future.
