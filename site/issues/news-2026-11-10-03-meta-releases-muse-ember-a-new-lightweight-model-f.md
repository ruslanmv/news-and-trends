## What Happened

Meta has released Muse Ember, a new lightweight model for agent execution. This model is significantly cheaper than its competitors, Ember and Luna, at 10 cents per million input tokens.

Muse Ember offers several benefits, including:

* Faster agent training
* Reduced resource consumption
* Improved security and privacy

The release of Muse Ember is a significant milestone for Meta, as the company aims to make its AI services more accessible to businesses of all sizes. The model is also expected to lower the cost of AI training, making it more affordable for startups and smaller businesses.
