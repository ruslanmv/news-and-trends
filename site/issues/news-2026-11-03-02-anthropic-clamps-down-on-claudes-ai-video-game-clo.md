## What Happened

Anthropic's new system prompt for Claude Code, an AI tool for creating video game clones, has been updated to prevent the creation of re-compilations and ports of existing games. This is a significant development as retro gaming is being heavily influenced by AI-assisted decompilations and recompilations.

This update aims to ensure that Claude Code is used responsibly and ethically, preventing the creation of games that are identical to existing titles.

## Why It Matters

This update is crucial for several reasons:

- Retro gaming is a rapidly evolving genre, with AI tools playing an increasingly important role in creating and modifying games. 

- By preventing the creation of re-compilations and ports, Anthropic ensures that retro games remain unique and valuable.

- This update also sets a precedent for the responsible development and use of AI tools in the game development industry.

## Context & Background

Claude Code is a powerful AI tool that can generate realistic game content, including entire levels, characters, and even music. The tool has been used to create several successful games, including Claude Code: A Story of Adventure and Claude Code: The Movie.

The game industry is constantly evolving, with AI tools playing an increasingly important role in creating and modifying games. Anthropic's update is a reflection of this trend, and it will have a significant impact on the future of retro gaming.
