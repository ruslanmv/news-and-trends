## What Happened

Google's Personal Intelligence feature, which lets Gemini pull data from apps like Google Photos to offer responses tailored to you, has now expanded to use that data and its Nano Banana 2 image model to create images based on your personal context. With this feature, you can use prompts like "Design my dream house" or "Create a [something that is relevant to your context]" to see various results generated by the AI.

## Why It Matters

This expansion is significant because it allows users to explore their creative potential in a whole new way. It's also a step forward for AI, demonstrating that it can use context and data to generate truly unique and personalized results. This could lead to a wide range of applications, from personalized education to design and marketing.

## Context & Background

The feature is the latest addition to Google's AI capabilities and has the potential to revolutionize the way people interact with technology. It's a significant step in the development of AI that can go beyond simple text-based responses and offer a more immersive and creative experience. It also highlights the potential of AI to be used for social good, by helping people to express themselves more freely and creatively.

## What to Watch Next

The feature is expected to launch in beta testing in the next few months, with a full rollout expected in the first half of 2027. This means that users can start experimenting with it soon and see how it impacts their creativity and personal expression.
