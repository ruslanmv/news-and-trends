## What Happened

The article announces the launch of a new AI-powered platform that can expand global access to reliable flood forecasts. This platform, called "FloodPredict," uses machine learning algorithms to analyze vast amounts of data from various sources, including weather patterns, satellite imagery, and hydrological data. By combining this data with advanced machine learning techniques, FloodPredict can generate highly accurate flood forecasts, even in areas that are traditionally difficult to monitor, such as remote or mountainous regions.

## Why It Matters

Flood prediction is critical for reducing human suffering and property damage caused by natural disasters. By providing timely and accurate flood forecasts, FloodPredict can help communities prepare for and respond to floods more effectively. This can save lives, protect infrastructure, and minimize economic losses.

## Context & Background

The announcement comes at a time when global climate change is increasingly leading to more frequent and severe floods. In 2022, the cost of floods exceeded $100 billion worldwide, and the number of flood-related deaths surpassed 2,000.

## What to Watch Next

FloodPredict is expected to launch its beta version within the next year and will be available to a limited number of users. The company plans to expand its reach to other countries in the coming years.
