## What Happened

Google has released a new commercial that imagines what the founding fathers would think about AI. The commercial features a montage of historical figures and uses AI tools to recreate scenes from the Declaration of Independence. The commercial ends with a voiceover that imagines what the founding fathers would think about AI.

## Why It Matters

The commercial is a powerful reminder of the transformative power of AI. It shows how AI can be used to bring historical figures and events to life in a way that is both entertaining and educational. The commercial also raises important questions about the ethical use of AI and the potential for AI to be used for malicious purposes.

## Context & Background

The commercial was released in response to the recent debate about the use of AI in education. The commercial features a diverse group of people using AI tools to learn about the founding fathers. The commercial also highlights the importance of AI in creating a more inclusive society.
