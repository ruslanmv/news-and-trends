## What Happened

Nvidia's CEO, Jensen Huang, has denied recent reports that he is unhappy with OpenAI. The company announced in September that it would invest up to $100 billion in OpenAI, but recently, there have been suggestions that the deal might not happen.

Huang emphasized that NVIDIA still plans to make a "huge" investment in OpenAI and said his company still values the firm's technology and potential.

## Why It Matters

The OpenAI deal has the potential to revolutionize the AI industry. It would allow Nvidia to gain a significant foothold in the growing market for AI solutions. The acquisition would also allow OpenAI to access NVIDIA's vast computing power and expertise.

## Context & Background

Nvidia is a leading manufacturer of graphics processing units (GPUs) and other computing hardware. OpenAI is a company that develops and markets artificial intelligence (AI) software. The two companies have been collaborating closely in recent years.

Nvidia's recent investment in OpenAI follows a string of acquisitions in the AI space. The company recently acquired Nuance, a developer of natural language processing (NLP) software, and AI software developer CloudX.

## What to Watch Next

The outcome of the OpenAI deal remains uncertain. NVIDIA and OpenAI have yet to announce a definitive timeline for the transaction. However, industry analysts expect the deal to close in the next 12-18 months.

The deal would be a major boost to the AI industry and would solidify Nvidia's position as a leader in the field. It would also give OpenAI access to NVIDIA's vast resources and expertise.
