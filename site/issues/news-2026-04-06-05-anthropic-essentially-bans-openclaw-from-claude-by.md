## What Happened

Anthropic has just implemented a new policy that will significantly impact Claude users. Starting April 4th at 3PM ET, users will no longer be able to use their Claude subscription limits for third-party harnesses including OpenClaw. This means that OpenClaw will no longer be able to function within the Claude ecosystem.

## Why It Matters

This change poses a significant challenge to Claude users who heavily rely on OpenClaw for their AI and data management needs. This decision was made due to Anthropic's ongoing effort to control third-party integrations and ensure the security and privacy of user data.

## Context & Background

Anthropic and Claude are both industry leaders in the field of AI and data management. Anthropic's OpenClaw is a popular tool for users to manage their AI and data resources across various platforms. Claude is a large online platform that allows users to connect and collaborate with other AI enthusiasts and professionals.

## What to Watch Next

The immediate impact of this policy change is that OpenClaw will no longer be accessible to Claude users. However, the long-term implications are more nuanced. While this decision may cause some inconvenience, it ultimately aligns with Anthropic's commitment to maintaining the security and privacy of its users.
